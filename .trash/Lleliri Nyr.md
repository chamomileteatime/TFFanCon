# Background
The Demi-Prince of Pond Fish
Race: Dunmer/Daedra, From Vvardenfell

Lleliri’s mother was a dissident priest and member of House Hlaalu. Some time after she was captured by the Tribunal Temple and placed within Baar Dau for reconditioning, she mysteriously gave birth to a healthy baby girl despite showing no signs of pregnancy. Wary of Daedric influences, the baby was taken to the Temple proper within Vivec City, where she was raised by Vivec’s clergy alongside other orphans who had the god’s interest. When the Tribunal Temple fell following the deaths of both Almalexia and Sotha Sil, many of these orphans fell under the jurisdiction of the Empire. Under Imperial Law, Lleliri would be considered not an “orphan” but a “victim of child trafficking.” As such, she was relocated out of Vvardenfell and into the Imperial City, where she fell in bad terms with the law several times in quick succession.
