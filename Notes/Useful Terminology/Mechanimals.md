## Mammalian Equivalents
### Turbofox
Animal Equivalent: Foxes & Wolves
Size: Knee-Height
Diet: Cyber-nymphs, Sparklets, Packs will attempt to eat a full-grown Cybertronian if starving.

<u>Information</u>
Cybertronian equivalent to a fox. Known for being faster than most all Cybertronians. Typically individualistic, but will roam in packs if resources are scarce.

<u>Appearance</u>
Extremely varied, almost as much as Cybertronians. Distinguishable by their long ears and large tails, as well as general body plan. Tend to become larger and more spikey when groups in packs, likely as some sort of dominance mechanism.

 <u>Reproduction</u>
 Like most Cybertronian wildlife, turbofoxes reproduce asexually through Budding. As a turbofox matures it grows in size, until eventually the creature reaches its reproductive size (around waist high) at which point it sheds a large amount of *sentio metallico*, which will eventually form several identical clones of the parent organism using its CNA.

<u>Relations with Cybertronians</u>
Roaming packs are frequently hunted and killed as quickly as possible, with only very few Cybertronians caring about how essential the turbofox population is to keeping the Cybertronian population in-check.

### Glitch-mice
Animal Equivalent: Rats & Mice
Size: Ankle-Sized
Diet: Energon

<u>Information</u>
Cybertronian equivalent to a mouse. Can release a painful zap that can cause complications for sick or injured Cybertronians. Live in large groups. Sleep by hanging upside-down from their tails from pipes or other forms of infrastructure.

<u>Appearance</u>
Rounder than the typical mouse, with a long tail more like a rat. A round glowing light at the end of their tail, like a lightbulb.

<u>Reproduction</u>
 Like most Cybertronian wildlife, glitch-mice reproduce asexually through Budding. As a glitch-mouse matures it grows in size, until eventually the creature reaches its reproductive size (around mid-calf) at which point it sheds a large amount of *sentio metallico*, which will eventually form several identical clones of the parent organism using its CNA. Glitch-mice are unique in that they shed their *entirety* when they reproduce, the parent organism dying in order to greatly increase the number of offspring created.
 
<u>Relations with Cybertronians</u>
Glitch-mice are very common in cities, where they feed from discarded energon or by chewing leaks into pipes. Viewed as a pest both for this and the painful zaps they give when grabbed.

### Cybercat
Animal Equivalent: Large Cats
Size: Thigh-Height
Diet: Cybernymphs, Sparklets, Turbofoxes

<u>Information</u>
Cybertronian equivalent of a large cat. The natural predator of the turbofox, and it’s significantly smarter cousin. Extremely individualistic. Nocturnal.

<u>Appearance</u>
Significantly more sleek than a turbofox, with a jaw shaped similarly to a staple-remover. Thin long tails. Typically darkly colors and monochrome, although brightly-colored cybercats can exist.

<u>Reproduction</u>
Like most Cybertronian wildlife, cybercats reproduce asexually through Budding. As a cybercat matures it grows in size, until eventually the creature reaches its reproductive size (around waist high) at which point it sheds a large amount of *sentio metallico*, which will eventually form several identical clones of the parent organism using its CNA.

<u>Relations with Cybertronians</u>
Cybercats will eat sparklets or cybernymphs when resources are scarce, but, as they are more intelligent that their turbofox counterparts, tend to stay away from Cybertronians when able to. This means that Cybertronians and cybercats tend to ignore one another, although cybercats attempting to get by in urban areas are frequently chased out for the sake of aesthetics and cleanliness.

## Reptile Equivalents
### Avianoids
Animal Equivalent: Birds and Bats, sometimes even pterosaurs
Size: Ankle-Height
Diet: Glitch-mice, Rustbugs

<u>Information</u>
The Cybertronian equivalent of most flying predators. Rather small and skittish, avianoids are typically diurnal, although crepuscular variants do exist. 

<u>Appearance</u>
Varied helm-structure, but the body plan is almost always the same, with two large plane-like wings on a small, rectangular body. Small pedes only suited for perching on the underside. 

<u>Reproduction</u>
Like most Cybertronian wildlife, avianoids reproduce asexually through Budding. As an avianoid matures it grows in size, until eventually the creature reaches its reproductive size (around waist high) at which point it sheds a large amount of *sentio metallico*, which will eventually form several identical clones of the parent organism using its CNA.

<u>Relations with Cybertronians</u>
As the main predator of the glitch-mouse, Cybertronians in urban areas are typically ambivalent to avianoid populations.

## Insect Equivalents
### Rustbugs
Animal Equivalent: Birds and Bats, sometimes even pterosaurs
Size: Finger-Sized
Diet: Energon

<u>Information</u>
The Cybertronian equivalent of an insect. Unlike most other mechanimals, the rustbug has a very primal transformation sequence, leading to them being the source of much study from Cybertronian environmentalists. Rather small and extremely skittish.

<u>Appearance</u>
Very similar to a sparklet, save for a much less developed helm structure. Transforms into a very simple ball structure which can roll away from predators.

<u>Reproduction</u>
Like most Cybertronian wildlife, rustbugs reproduce asexually through Budding. As an rustbug matures it grows in size, until eventually the creature reaches its reproductive size (around the size of a servo) at which point it sheds a large amount of *sentio metallico*, which will eventually form several identical clones of the parent organism using its CNA.

<u>Relations with Cybertronians</u>
As the main predator of the glitch-mouse, Cybertronians in urban areas are typically ambivalent to avianoid populations.

## Other
### Scraplets
Animal Equivalent: N/A
Size: Hand-Sized
Diet: Energon

<u>Information</u>
Scraplets operating as a hive, similar to bees or ants. They typically build their nests in populated areas, attacking any Cybertronian that comes near. The Cybertronian’s energon is used to feed their hive for months at a time, while their plating and internals are melted down by the Scraplet’s acidic mouths, and used to expand the hive. 

<u>Reproduction</u>
Scraplet queens are born from hotspots, the same as Cybertronians. Queen nymphs function similarly to cuckoos, in that their nymph state is indistinguishable from a cybernymph except for being slightly larger and faster. This allows them free reign to gorge themselves on cybernymphs until they grow large enough to molt into a proper scraplet queen, and begin building their hive. 

Scraplet drones are born form a queen in waves. Scraplet queens grow continuously; constantly pushing out *sentio metallico* as they do. 

<u>Relations with Cybertronians</u>
Greatly feared by Cybertronians, primarily for how quickly a large pack of them can rip a Cybertronian down to material components. Some studies potentially prove that Scraplet Queens are pushed out of hotspots as a natural population control for Cybertronians, but this is extremely contested.
