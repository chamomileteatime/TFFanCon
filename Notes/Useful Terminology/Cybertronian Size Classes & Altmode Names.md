---
## Minicon – 15ft
About waist-height on Standard.
## Minibot – 20ft
A bit under chest-height on Standard.
## Standard – 30ft
The standard size of a Cybertronian. 

## Jet-Size – 35ft
Only slightly larger than Standard.

## Heavyweight – 40ft
About a head and shoulders taller than Standard.

## Titan-Class – Unstandardized
Titan-specific size-class. Too varied to be standardized. Typically around 700ft.

## Midweight
A term for when a Cybertronian falls somewhere between two size classes. Uncommon. Typically a sign of some sort of abnormality, such as being a point-one percenter, or having a non-standard altmode.


# Common Altmode Names
Altmodes with standardized Cybertronian names that do not match the name of the vehicle.
## Grounder Altmodes
### Speedster
Altmode Type: Any extremely fast car. Considered at least vaguely different from the standard car altmode?
### Convoy
Altmode Type: Semi-Trucks with Trailers.

## Flying Altmodes
### Seekers
Altmode Type: A very specific kind of standardized cold-constructed jet. More than just a name for a specific altmode. (see: [[Generation Two]])

### Rotaries
Altmode Type: Helicopters and any sort of flying vehicle with rotary blades. 

## Other Altmodes
### Datasticks
Altmode Type: USB sticks.

### Carriers (Sometimes Called “Cassette Carriers” Specifically)
Altmode Type: Cassette players, boomboxes, anything that fits cassettes.
