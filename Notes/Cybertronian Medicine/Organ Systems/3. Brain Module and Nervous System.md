## Brain Module Function
### Primary Regions of the Brain module
**Trixiomatic:** Believed to house social connection, recognition, sympathy, inhibition, relationships with attention and interconnectedness, inter- and intra-personal dynamics, and a big-picture view of society. Most psychiatric diagnoses are believed to be the fault of some error in this region, and this is the site of most documented shadowplay.
- **Superior Inframatum:** Moral reasoning, emotional control, dependency; one of the most prominent exterior ports of the brain module, it has a very impulsive connection to the frame's motor functions.

**Lexiomatic:** Language processing, identification/categorization, understanding word/speech associations and connotation

**Parietal:** Sensory recepticon and processing, bodily awareness (proprioception), fine motor control, procedural memory and habits
- Most exterior nodes are also connected, even if just barely, to the parietal region. This is also what allows for dextrous use of transformation and secondary anatomy.

**Engramatic:** Executes commands to retrieve explicit long-term memories. Memory is not centered in this region, it's spread out; this region is just the most adept at retrieving long-term memories from deeper within the brain module. 

**Sentiomatic:** Strong emotional processing, aggression, anger, deep empathy, arousal, fear, enjoyment, pathological associations and emotional conditioning and regulation

**Bathium Core:** Formed the earliest during forging/development; contains an interior ego/sense of self, allows frame to recognize itself as a whole person, contains deeply-imbedded triggers and concepts. Does not necessarily mean this is where "identity" is---this is thought to be where the sense of *being* comes from, and is just commonly misunderstood to be synonymous with identity.
