# Introduction to Cybertronian anatomy
### Summary of Anatomical Systems

| Category             | Energy                                                                    | Fuel                                      | Transformation                                                       | Processing                                                                                       | Protection                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Related Organs/Parts | Spark, spark casing, alternator (+ turbine) and generator(s), power banks | Fuel pump, fuel tanks, fuel lines         | Transformation cog, transformation seams, secondary anatomy/"kibble" | Brain module, life cord (spinal strut/cranial stem), sensors and processors, optics              | Outer plating, skeleton, immune system (nanites and regulators), ventilation |
| Function             | Recharging, sleep/wake cycles, providing energy                           | Providing energon, storing unused energon | Ease of movement, transformation                                     | Consciousness, main 8 senses, control/awareness of body, memory storage, pain reception, emotion | External protection, response against disease/injury, temperature regulation |

Cybertronians group the three primary functions of the body into three systems centered around their respective principal organs: the **spark**, the **brain module**, and the **transformation cog**. 
> Only the spark is absolutely necessary for the Cybertronian to be considered "alive", but some may consider destruction of the brain module to be sufficient instead. 
> While sparks and brain modules are significantly more difficult to repair (and impossible to seamlessly replace), transformation cogs can be worked on.

Beyond the main three, Cybertronians are much more durable than the average organic species and capable of surviving with underfunctioning organs. Most frames have built-in backup generators and power banks, and most organs sans the spark and brain module can be easily replaced.

### Basic Makeup
The Cybertronian body is layered; **exterior plating** comes first, then interior/**basic plating**, then the **protoform** (a catch-all term for soft tissue), then the skeletal system. The skeletal system is not absolutely mandatory for some movement, but it is important to *have* else the transformation system will break down.

The layout of a Cybertronian's frame, including its alt mode, is all predetermined by a spark's encoded **CNA** (or cybernanoalgorithm). Under the right circumstances, a spark's CNA may be modified before it even begins to form a body; **radiation** is a theorized risk factor in creating **outlier** and **green** (0.1%) sparks, but it hasn't been proven beyond a reasonable doubt.
