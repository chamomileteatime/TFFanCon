## Introduction: immature Life Stages
The most drastic changes to a Cybertronian happen in the three “Sparklet” lifestages. In this period a Cybertronian will go through 90% of the growth they will experience in their entire life. 

The length of the Sparklet stages depend heavily on the weight-class of the Cybertronian. The smaller the final product, the quicker the process can be completed.

### Cyber-Nymphs
Nymphs are the smallest stage of Cybertronian life. They are marked by a complete lack of coloration or kibble. Nymphs lack complex intelligence, and therefore rarely stray from the hotspot of their birth. They are barely more than an exposed spark-casing with legs, leading to this lifestage being by far the deadliest. Many creatures on Cybertron, such as turbofoxes and scraplets, rely on cyber-nymphs as a major fuel-source as they are rather small and plentiful.

### Sparklets
Color begins to form along the protoform in abnormal spots, typically starting on the back and face and progressing to the belly overtime. This increase in nanite production marks the start of the sparklet stage.

The sparklet stage is significantly longer than both the nymph and molting stages, second in length only to a fully-matured Cybertronian. The exact length of this lifestage depends on many factors, such as CNA differences, size-class, and available resources. Cybertronians of an average size-class or higher can remain in this stage for up to 1.0mil years, should resources remain too tight. For a Titan-Class Cybertronian, 1.0mil years is the *minimum* growth time. 

Kibble development begins in the sparklet stage as well. Kibble placement may be abnormal—it is important to note that the sparklet’s exterior develops into the full-formed Cybertronian’s *altmode*, **not** their rootmode. This is excluding the helm, which does not properly retract until molting, when the sparklet will begin to develop a separate sensory suite specific to their altmode. Certain types of kibble will develop fully in this stage, but most parts do not reach full development until the molting stage.

The sparklet stage ends when the sparklet reaches the full size of their altmode, at which point the molting stage will begin rapidly

### Molting
Molting is the shortest lifestage of a Cybertronian, only taking around a year, regardless of size. The molting stage technically begins well before proper molting, at the point where a sparklet reaches the full size of its altmode. At this point, the sparklet will begin to experience an extreme desire to eat, often tearing through not just energon reserves but many kinds of metal necessary to plate development as well. The sparklet will also completely loose the desire and need for recharge, focusing entirely on procuring enough fuel and materials to complete it’s development.


Layers of plate metal quickly begin to grow over the protoform, restricting movement. Molting-specific nanite development begins under the protective layer, slowly carving away at the protoform and fusing it to sections of the outer layer. As this happens, internal repair nanites finish the transformation of the sparklet’s internal systems into those resembling a fully-matured Cybertronian. Eventually, when both internal and external development are completed, excess plating will be shed, and a fully-constructed altmode will emerge. 

## True Cybertronians
Once the molting stage has completed, and the protective casing has been shed, the Cybertronian will complete its first transformation into its rootmode, marking the end of it’s development. From this point on, the Cybertronian will cease to grow completely, and any later changes or additions will require a full operation.

## Other: Cold-Constructs

Cold-Constructed Cybertronians (see: [[Cold Constructs]]) lack any true “sparklet” stages, instead being constructed directly into their matured lifestage. It remains unstudied if this has any affect on the emotional subroutines of Cold-Constructs, nor if it affects their physical prowess. It is widely assumed that this may contributes to a lesser sense of dexterity in Cold-Constructs.

## Other: Growth Errors
### Trauma
Trauma in nymph stage can cause certain kinds of kibble to develop early. This includes siren, wheels, or anything else that could be used as a form of defense. As a consequence of this, however, these early-developed Cybertronians often retain certain sparklet features well into adulthood, such as improper segmentation or abnormal intake shapes.
