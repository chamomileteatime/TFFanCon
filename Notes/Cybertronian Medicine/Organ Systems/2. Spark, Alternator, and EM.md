## Introduction
Sparks are the center of any living, functioning Cybertronian. Not only are they responsible for containing a mech's genetic code as the spark's own **CNA**, but the spark's organ system allows power to be distributed throughout the frame, and the spark's **frequency** determines the efficiency and functioning of all other anatomical components.

If we’re to think of the circulatory system as what moves the fuel around, think of the spark and its adjacent organ system as what makes this fuel useful in the first place. Whereas humans must oxygenate their blood through the lungs, Cybertronians must empower their energon by way of their sparks.

Sparks also directly control sleep-wake cycles, drift velocity and charge density (analogous to blood pressure), and the dispersal of hormone-like chemicals depending on a Cybertronian’s emotional reactions.

## The Nature of a Spark
Sparks are mostly balls of plasma composed of positrons. Positrons respond negatively to being introduced to their matter equivalents, electrons, so the spark protects itself by engineering an **electromagnetic (EM) field** to regulate the energy exchange between itself and the rest of the body. 

The EM field is further reinforced by the **spark chamber**. With a dysregulated or broken chamber (see: [[Spark Chamber Malformation and Absense]], the EM field may waver, making any trauma inflicted upon the spark chamber an upmost priority in emergency medicine.

### Spark Types
Sparks are designated by three main types, but the "spark type" actually moreso refers to the state of the energon---specifically, the way in which the spark modifies the empowered energon. Spark types are additionally distinguished as being either positive or negative, referring to the orientation of the spark's spin (positive being clockwise, negative being counterclockwise).

The three spark types are
#### Ferrum / Adaptive
**Universal recipient**; the most common type. Refers to a spark type that can adapt itself to different frequencies and environments especially easily. Very rare to ever be in the alt modes described under isomeric's criteria.
#### Isomeric / Composite
**Universal donor**; refers to a spark type that is comprised of many different elements and capable of delivering a complex range of charge to a frame. This type is more common in triple-changers/0.1%ers, outliers, larger frames, and frames with large amounts of natural integrated tools (i.e. ambulances).
#### Vitreous / Static
**Most rare**; refers to a spark type that is void of complications/"impurities". Surprisingly, this spark type is valued because it's more rare and easier to manage, not because it's more "pure". This type is more common in scientific alt modes.

## Energy Conservation and Recharge
**Recharging** is the equivalent of “sleeping”. In order to properly power the body, a Cybertronian has to recharge on average every **10-14 Earth days**. Some can go longer without and some require more. While recharge sessions typically last around 12 Earth hours, this also varies from person to person just as human circadian rhythms do. Cybertronians with immobile alt modes tend to require less recharge, while grounded vehicles tend to require more.

Sleep deprivation (or **recharge deprivation**, in this instance) gradually decreases the amount of power energized into the frame’s fuel. This can manifest as **fatigue**, **dim optics/biolights**, **sluggish movements**, and **slower reflexes**.

If deprived of recharge for too long, the frame resorts to pulling power from its **power banks**. While this means that staying awake for too long doesn’t tend to be fatal, **putting too much stress on the body’s power banks is ill-advised** and could result in wearing them down (see: [[Power Bank and Battery Dysfunctions]]. It’s better in the long run to just recharge as the frame needs, especially because power banks are very short-term solutions and will eventually be emptied.


## Fuel Energization: Alternator and Generator
**Alternators**, whose turbine shafts are directly controlled by the spark/spark chamber, put “power” into the energon the same way lungs put oxygen into human blood. This process is referred to as fuel energization. Depending on the frequency of the spark, the alternator will become proportionally more/less efficient.

Alternators have one main input (a channel through the turbine shaft to the spark chamber), two main outputs (two power banks), and a canal-like passage connected to the fuel pump where the frame’s energon flows through. 

Alternators are also lined with **voltage regulators** responsible for keeping a Transformer from putting out too much energy into the body’s energon, and are attached to a wide array of fuel lines which allow it to receive its own power so that it works in the first place.

Alternators are also connected to a **backup generator**; most Cybertronians have these **redundant** (ie. performing a function already there), inactive organs only present in the event that the alternator is unable to continue working. Alternators are typically able to self-repair (as are backup generators), so this isn’t a common issue.

A malfunctioning alternator results in lower power production, which in turn prompts the frame to reroute most of its power to “**essential systems**”. Organ systems with preferential treatment here are the spark/chamber, the fuel pump, the brain module, and the relevant transformation seams. All other functions are deemed secondary.

The most common issue for alternators to experience has to deal with their input fuel lines. When the fuel lines are clogged by the frame’s **nanites** (immune response microbots), the alternator struggles to receive as much power, and in turn struggles to put out the power it needs to give the frame’s energon. This issue is easily solved by **prescription dispersants**, but if clots form or the alternator begins to experience structural damage after being cut off from power, more invasive treatment (surgery, worst case an [[Alternectomies|alternectomy]]) may be required.
