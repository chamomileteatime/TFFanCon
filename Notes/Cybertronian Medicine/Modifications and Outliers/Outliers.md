## In Cold-Construction
Contrary to popular belief, outlier abilities in reality can be more common in Cold-Constructs than in forged Cybertronians. This is a side-effect of the spark-splicing process in which abnormal abilities and mutations become more frequent the more often a spark-splice is reused to ignite new sparks.
## Known Outlier Abilities
### Skids of Nova Chronum — Hypercompetence
*Source of Ability:* Parietal Region of the Brain Module
While Skids’ ability *presents* itself as the ability to pick up any skill without need for repetition, through research it has been concluded that his ability bypasses the part of the brain responsible for learning and memory entirely. One can only assume his ability must then operate by allowing him to subconsciously rewrite sections his own brain module to suit his needs. 
#### Secondary Mutations
*Achieved?* No
Based on the core function of Skids’ outlier ability, the most likely secondary mutation would be developing the ability to rewrite and edit other sections of his brain module in the same way, or, in other words, extending beyond just being able to teach himself skills, but invent new skills on the fly.

#### Skywarp — Teleportation
*Source of Ability:* Unique Anatomy (Warp Core)
Skywarp’s outlier ability grants him the power of teleportation, using a unique system in his chassis which functions similarly to a spacebridge. Unlike a spacebridge, however, the teleportation is instantaneous, with no visible signs other than a flash of purple light. Skywarp is also capable of teleporting others within a small range of him, which seems to be tied with the scope of his EMF. Despite its surface-level simplicity, teleportations is perhaps one of the least understood outlier abilities.
##### Secondary Mutations
Achieved? No.
A secondary mutation to Skywarp’s ability would likely present itself as an increased range of teleportation, or the development of the ability to teleport others without needed to teleport himself. Some particularly “out there” outlier researchers have concluded that it could be possible for his ability to develop into something that could manipulate time as well as space, but this is highly unlikely.
