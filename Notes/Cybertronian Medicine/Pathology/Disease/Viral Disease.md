**Viruses** are very simple pockets of CNA that have the ability to infect and reproduce inside host technoform. They are not sentient, not considered living, extremely small, and occasionally useful.

## Taxonomy
The viral taxonomy was first drafted by [[Helminth]] during Cybertron's Golden Age. Modern developments have led to several revisions; the current system, edited by [[Pharma]], remains the research standard.

#### REALM
1. **Niduviria**, *describing viruses originating from hotspot malformations*
2. **Mudoviria**, *describing viruses originating from protoform development
3. **Cynaviria**, *describing viruses originating from sudden malignant episodes*

Cybertronian viruses are often simple, short-lived, and very difficult to identify before they're killed by either the host body or environmental hazards. For this reason, viral taxonomy remains remarkably condensed.

## Significant Known Viruses

### REALM: NIDUVIRIA
#### TAXONOMY
**REALM:** *Niduviria*
- **KINGDOM:** *Vibavirae* (alchemical viruses)
	- **CLASS:** *Aptoviricetes* (a class of bacteriophage)
	- **CLASS:** *Perdeviricetes* (a class of highly reactive viruses)
		- **GENUS:** *Rubrevirus* (a fast-and-fatal multi-system rust-inducing virus)

#### DESCRIPTION
Viruses categorized under *Niduviria* are large, short-lived, and tend to thrive in extreme conditions. They're hypothesized to come right out of hotspots or other places where sparks would emerge; instead of encoding a spark with CNA, the hotspot/place of origin produces an enclosed packet of stray CNA carrying enough instruction to attach itself to a host (usually a grub).

Viruses categorized under *Niduviria* *can* be artificially produced without a hotspot, and in fact, one of the major complications of experiments attempting to induce spliced-sparks with outlier traits is the risk of splitting off an immature virion. Because of this, *Niduviria* are suspected to come not from the hotspots themselves, but from the growth/death of a spark only witnessed upon forging/splicing or in specific conditions i.e. acute [[Cybercrosis]].

### REALM: CYNAVIRIA
#### TAXONOMY
**REALM:** *Cynaviria*
- **KINGDOM:** *Instruvirae* (procedural viruses)
	- **CLASS:** *Soviricetes* (a class of neurophysiological viruses)
		- **GENUS:** *Fabrillovirus* (ex. Shockwave's manufactured instructional virus)
		- **GENUS:** *Eidedovirus* (ex. a hypothesized origin of [[Information Creep and Eidetic Decay|information creep]])
	- **CLASS:** *Verteviricetes* (a class of order-shifting viruses)
		- **GENUS:** *Compleuravirus* (inc. *Complecipleuravirus pandemicum*, classically known as [[Chromatic Fever]])

#### DESCRIPTION
Viruses categorized under *Cynaviria* reproduce extremely quickly and in large numbers. They can be, but do not have to be, significantly smaller than a host's nanites, and can multiply especially freely in the brain module / life cord.

*Cynaviria* is of unique significance to mnemonologists and mnemosurgeons, as interfacing with a brain module highly infected with a virus belonging to *Soviricetes* risks the surgeon contracting the virus themself. 
Chronic infection via some undocumented virus is hypothesized to be the reason mnemosurgeons die young; arguments have been made blaming mnemosurgery addiction not on the act of injecting itself, but on dependence on a specific code generated by *Soviricetes*.

Are the Decepticons using viruses to mind-control their soldiers? It's not completely impossible, according to Autobot scientists.

Perhaps the most infamous example of a virus belonging to *Cynaviria* is *Complecipleuravirus pandemica* -- it even has its own page [[Chromatic Fever|right here]]. **Chromatic Fever** was responsible for killing a large majority of Cybertron's population from its onset at the end of the Golden Age to the turn of the Modern Era.
