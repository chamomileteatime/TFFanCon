**Viruses** are very simple pockets of CNA that have the ability to infect and reproduce inside host technoform. 

## Taxonomy
The viral taxonomy was first drafted by [[Helminth]] during Cybertron's Golden Age. Modern developments have led to several revisions; the current system, authored by [[Pharma]], remains the research standard.

#### REALM
1. **Niduviria**, *describing viruses originating from hotspot malformations*
2. **Mudoviria**, *describing viruses originating from protoform development
3. **Cynaviria**, *describing viruses originating from sudden malignant episodes*

Cybertronian viruses are often simple, short-lived, and very difficult to identify before they're killed by either the host body or environmental hazards. For this reason, viral taxonomy remains remarkably condensed.

## Significant Known Viruses

### REALM: NIDUVIRIA
#### TAXONOMY
**REALM:** *Niduviria*
- **KINGDOM:** *Vibavirae* (alchemical viruses)
	- **CLASS:** *Aptoviricetes* (a class of bacteriophage)
	- **CLASS:** *Perdeviricetes* (a class of highly reactive viruses)
		- **GENUS:** *Rubrevirus* (a fast-and-fatal multi-system rust-inducing virus)

#### DESCRIPTION
Viruses categorized under *Niduviria* are large, short-lived, and tend to thrive in extreme conditions. They're hypothesized to come right out of hotspots or other places where sparks would emerge; instead of encoding a spark with CNA, the hotspot/place of origin produces an enclosed packet of stray CNA carrying enough instruction to attach itself to a host (usually a grub).

Viruses categorized under *Niduviria* *can* be artificially produced without a hotspot, and in fact, one of the major complications of experiments attempting to induce spliced-sparks with outlier traits is the risk of splitting off an immature virion. Because of this, *Niduviria* are suspected to come not from the hotspots themselves, but from the growth/death of a spark only witnessed upon forging/splicing or in specific conditions i.e. acute [[Cybercrosis]].
