# Psychiatric Notes
## Ableism on Cybertron
### “Conduct” Disorders
In Cybertronian medicine, most mental health issues are still considered to be some sort of “conduct” disorder, with medical understanding being limited to :this person has some sort of protocol malady limiting their ability to serve their intended function.” Cybertron is by no means advanced when it comes to mental health.
## protocol maladies
*Note: Due to the typical behavior of Cybertronians, there is not an equivalent diagnosis for many parts of the DSM-5, such as Autism Spectrum Disorders.*
### Conduct Disorders
#### Cold Conduct Disorders (cCd)
Categorized as the more introverted of the Conduct Disorders.
##### Lethargic CCD
Cybertronian equivalent of *Clinical Depression*. Defined as someone who struggles to keep up with daily expectations and basic hygiene, often alongside unexpected emotional outbursts.
##### Anxious HCD
Cybertronian equivalent of *Generalized Anxiety Disorder*, some *Obsessive-Compulsive Disorders*,  and *Panic Disorder*. Defined as someone who sustains intense or disproportionate emotional responses to stressful stimuli.
##### Paranoid ccd
Cybertronian equivalent of *Paranoid Personality Disorder*, some *Obsessive-Compulsive Disorders*, and *Phobias*. Defined as someone who exhibits regular and extreme boughs of paranoia. Typically seen as a more severe version of Anxious HCD.
##### Rigid CCD
Cybertronian equivalent of *Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder*. Defined as someone who struggles outside of highly rigid environments, and is completely unable to function without clear rules or guidance. 
##### egocentric ccd
Cybertronian equivalent of *Narcissistic Personality Disorder*. Defined as someone who centers themself over the needs of others or Cybertron as a whole. Can either present as someone highly egocentric or someone with a strong need for external validation.
#####  classical ccd
Cybertronian equivalent of *Anti-Social Personality Disorder*. Defined as someone who disregards both other’s needs as well as rules and responsibilities.
#### Hot Conduct Disorders (hcd)
Categorized as the more extroverted of the Conduct Disorders.
##### Hyperactive hcd
Cybertronian equivalent of *Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder*. Defined as someone who struggles to work for even short periods of time due to an excess of energy.
##### Classical hcd
Cybertronian equivalent of *Borderline Personality Disorder*, *Avoidant Personality Disorder*, and  *Dependent Personality Disorder*. Defined as someone who relies on others for emotional regulation, and struggles to function without external relationships.
##### classical-like HCD
Cybertronian equivalent of *Histrionic Personality Disorder*. Defined as someone who portrays themselves very eccentrically, alongside an ability to gauge appropriate social response.

#### Abnormal conduct disorders (ACD)
##### Hot-Cold Conduct Disorder
Cybertronian equivalent of *Bipolar Disorder*. Defined as someone who experience the symptoms of Lethargic CCD alongside episodes of extreme energy and poor decision-making.
##### Classical ACD
Cybertronian equivalent of *Schizophrenia*. Defined as anyone who regularly experiences both delusions and visual and/or audial hallucinations.
##### Moderate ACD
Cybertronian equivalent of *Schizotypal Personality Disorder*. Defined as anyone who regularly experiences delusions, but without visual or audial hallucinations.
##### Double-Abnormal ACD
Cybertronian equivalent of *Schizoaffective Disorder*. Defined as anyone who experiences **both** Hot-Cold Conduct Disorder and Classical ACD.
