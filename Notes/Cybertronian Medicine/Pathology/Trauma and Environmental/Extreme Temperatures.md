### Exposure to Cold
Prolonged exposure to the cold can wear down the Cybertronian's energy system, and especially the cold-susceptible power banks (energy storage). Because their ability to store power is hindered, their need for recharge is greatly increased, and this can't be remedied without replacing the power banks themselves. If exposed to the cold for long enough, if the body is unable to store enough charge to consistently supply the rest of the frame with enough, other organ systems may begin to suffer.
