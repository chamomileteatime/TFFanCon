# Deviations in Cybertronian Biology
## Spark Deviations
Spark Deviations are significantly more common that Frame Deviations, yet are also much less understood. The nature of the spark is still a mystery to Cybertron in many ways, making comprehensive study of what causes these abnormalities a near-impossibility.

### Forged Bonds
#### Split-Spark Bonds
Split-spark bonds are somewhat common, at least for an abnormality, but yet remain highly misunderstood. Part of this comes from the Cybertronian distaste for attachment, as split-spark twins are forged as attached to one another as any Cybertronian could ever be.

Split-sparks function similarly to combiner sparks, just without the actual ability to combine. They often present a low-level telepathic connection, and injuries which affect one spark affect both. This makes spark-stabilization even more important when dealing with split-spark twins, as if both are injured their sparks can rapidly devolve into a negative feedback loop of complications. Spark-death in split-spark bonds almost always occurs together, even should the uninjured party remain completely unharmed.

#### Combiners
##### Traditional Combiners
Traditional combiners are by no means common, but just about every Cybertronian has heard of them, at one point or another. Traditional combiners take the form of five (or occasionally six) Cybertronians which combine into one larger rootmode, roughly the size of a Titan. This transformation is separate from their actual altmode, save for the fact that the limbs of the combined form typically attach using their altmode rather than their rootmode.

Traditional combiners are perhaps the most studied and experimented form of abnormality, as their use on the battlefield cannot be overstated. Despite this, attempts at fully-functional created combiners have never been successful, often missing key parts such as the combiner bond or separately functional altmodes. Perhaps the most pathetic attempts in recent history include the Decepticon’s attempts to simply modify five Cybertronians to have anatomical altmodes, and simply attach them together.

##### Duocombiners
Unlike traditional combiners, duocombiners are made up of two smaller-than-average Cybertronians which combine into a form that is still within a standardized size. Duocombiners from a psychological standpoint function very similarly to split-spark twins, and there are some duocombiners which prefer to exist in their combined form first, and as separate beings only when absolutely necessary. Some scientists believe that there may be a deeper level of connection between split-sparks and duocombiners, and whether or not the difference is purely anatomical rather than anything deeper.

Duocombiners can take several different forms, such as two rootmodes which combine to one altmode, one rootmode which can split into two smaller rootmodes but only has one altmode, and two altmodes which split from a single rootmode. It’s all very confusing.

##### Headmasters
Headmasters, while typically sorted alongside other combiners, technically does not meet the base criteria for a combiner bond. Unlike all other forms of combination, Headmasters are one single spark split between two bodies, one of which is capable of slotting into the other. In this way, they are technically a form of “load-bearer,” although there are some that argue that Headmasters might represent a missing link between combiner sparks and Point One Percenters.

### Point One Percenters
Point One Percenters are the rarest form of abnormality that can occur during the forging process. They can be extremely dangerous to approach while being formed within a hotspot. This is because their formation often halts all other spark production, fusing all the energy produced by the hotspot into one single bright green spark. This fusion can result in a potentially deadly release of energy when the spark ceases formation. Sometimes also called “Once-A-Generation” Cybertronians.

#### Heavyweights
Most common type of Point One Percenter. Includes all Cybertronians forged at the 12m (40ft) height class, regardless of altmode. Heavyweights have a significantly higher chance of being a larger altmode, such as a Convoy or a Shuttle. Alongside their unique size, heavyweights are also abnormally strong and durable, making them key fighters on the battlefield.

#### Load-Bearers
Load-Bearers are an extremely rare form of Point One Percenter, a category which is already rare in its own right. Rather than having abnormally large frames like their brethren, load-bearers are most frequently forged at the Minicon size class. This difference in spark and frame allows them to augment their frame in a way possible to no other Cybertronian, even allowing them to wear a size-class of modified armor equal in size to their heavyweight counterparts. It is important that Load-Bearers are always properly treated from their irreducible form outwards, as their core-most frame is the only one directly connected to their internal systems. 

### Outliers
Outliers are the catch-all term for spark deviations which neither attach several Cybertronians to one another nor create the noticeably green glow of a Point One Percenter. Because of this, Outliers are frequently written off as “unexplainable magic” by the non-scientifically minded. While attempts to further categorize outliers have been made, exceptions to any rule appear near immediately, making rigorous study near impossible.

## Frame Deviations
### Midweights
Midweights are the most common form of frame deviation. Cybertronians are forged at extremely regulated size classes, each typically about 2m apart. Some Cybertronians, however, are forged outside of these standardized height classes. 

A Midweight is often sorted into different size-classes depending on context, but from a medical perspective, it is important to treat midweights as a deviation. This is because midweights often have different power loads than normal size-classes, as well as having raised chances of having anatomical anomalies, such as abnormal organ placements.

### Titans
Titans, while technically not an abnormality in Cybertronian anatomy, are rare enough to fall under the category of frame deviations, from a medical standpoint. Contrary to uneducated belief, Titans are not just standard-weight Cybertronians scaled up to an abnormal degree, but rather possess completely different anatomical systems, with many essential organ functions being larger and more complex than the entire standard-class Cybertronian frame. In the past, when Titans where significantly more common, specialized medics spent their entire lifespans studying and working with singular titans. Nowadays, most medical research on Titans is completely lost, making any attempts at medical assistance extremely dangerous and complex.

#### Cityspeakers
Cityspeakers are a widely misunderstood form of abnormal development. Assumptions can be made that it likely falls under some form of spark deviation or forged bond, but due to the near-extinction of Titan-class Cybertronians actual discoveries of Cityspeaker abilities have fallen nearly to zero. All that is known about them from more archaic records is that Cityspeakers were able of forming bonds with specific Titan-class Cybertronians, being able to communicate with them even while dormant. Some sources claim that a Cityspeaker could even control a Titan against their will, leading to some believing that the discovery of one such Cybertronian could lead to a major turning point in the war.

### Multi-Changers
Multi-changers come in many forms, but the basics of the group are that they are Cybertronians which possess more than one altmode. Triple-changers are by far the most common form of multi-changers, with quadruple-changers making up a small minority. Anything more than three altmodes has never been documented, the assumption being that the amount of kibble and transformation seams necessary for more than three altmodes is simply not feasible from an anatomical standpoint. Quadruple changers are already significantly more prone to issues regarding transformation. 

### Monoformers
Monoformers are medically differentiated between those with kibble and those without. Those with kibble are often given surgical modifications to transition them to a kibble-appropriate altmode as soon as possible. Monoformers without any form of kibble are extremely rare, and therefore there is no standardized path for medical assistance.

## Other Abnormalities
### Mass-Shifting
An ability that a Cybertronian is either forged with, or not. Allows a Cybertronian to transform into an altmode which does not match the size-class of their root mode. Mass-shifting raises in likelihood the smaller the size-class of the altmode. It was once extremely prevalent amongst the now mostly extinct Data-Caste.

## Notable examples
### Damus of Tarn / Glitch / Tarn
Damus was forged both a Point One Percenter and an Outlier. Some confusion was created early in his development, as outlier abilities are categorized specifically as the absence of a Point One Percenter spark. Some theorized that his abilities may reflect an extremely rare form of Point One Percenter, rather than being an outlier at all.

However, the two spark abnormalities seemed to be in great conflict with one another, causing issues in his development as well as potentially causing the use of his outlier to hurt him. Later in life as Tarn, Damus went through a successful reformat into a heavyweight-class frame with no issues, proving that his abnormally small stature was a consequence of improper development, rather than any unique form of Point One Percenter ability.
