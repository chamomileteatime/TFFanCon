# General Anatomical Terms
**Struts:** Bones
**Armor/Plating:** Hardest, Outermost layer of the “skin.” Not alive in the same way as the Protoform (similar to keratin).
**Kibble:** Anatomical features which serve no purpose in root-mode but are necessary for alt-mode function.
**Protoform:** “Insides;” Softer Metal Form beneath the armor plating of all Cybertronians, includes organs and struts. Vaguely humanoid, looks almost identical across all models (no kibble).
**Nanites:** Small, cell-like organisms which come in several forms.
**Paint Nanites:** Nanites which serve as a natural paint for Cybertronians. Grown naturally and survive on spark energy
**Repair Nanites:** Nanites which can be injected to heal the injured. Grown in a lab.

# Internal Terminology
**Processor:** General term for any part of the system capable of “thinking.” More broad and all-inclusive than Brain Module.
**Brain Module:** Brain
**CNA/Coding:** DNA/Genes
**Protocols:** Instincts, more typically used in functionist ideas
**Fuel Lines/ Energon Lines:** Veins
**Line Energon:** Blood
**Tanks:** Stomach
**Vents:** Breathing Apparatus (External and Internal)
**Spark:** Heart, but also kinda a soul? Fulfills both a literal and spiritual purpose culturally
**Sparkchamber:** Metal Sphere with contains the Spark
**Transformation Cog (T-Cog):** Transformation organ. Central to almost all movement, similar too but more than a spine.
**Biolights:** Small Lights scattered across the form
**Voxcoder/Voxbox:** Voicebox

# Head Region
**Helm:** Head (_typically_ does not include face)
**Audials:** Ears
**Finials:** Ear-fins (seen on mechs such as Optimus Prime)
**Audial Horns:** Horns (seen on mechs such as Jazz)
**Chevron:** Horn-like growth on front of face (seen on mechs such as Prowl)
**Mouthplate:** Non-removable mask (seen on mechs such as Soundwave)
**Battlemask:** Retractible mask (seen on mechs such as Optimus Prime)
**Faceplate:** Face
**Optics:** Eyes
**Lenses:** Glass covering the optical sensors that is part of the optics
**Visor:** Visor
**Optical Ridge:** Eyebrows (careful with this term: many mechs don’t have visible eyebrows)
**Intake:** Mouth
**Derma:** Lips
**Denta(e):** Teeth
**Glossa:** Tongue

# **Chest & Back Regions**
**Chassis:** Upper Chest/Thorax
**Bumper:** “Breasts??” (seen on mechs such as Prowl or Jazz) Seems to be used derogatorily?
**Core:** Lower Chest/Stomach
**Dorsal Plating:** Back
**Backstrut:** Spine
**Wings:** Flier-Specific Wing-Kibble (see: Doorwings)
**Ailerons:** Flappy bits on the far end of the wing when in alt-mode
**Hinges:** Connect the wings to the dorsal plating
**Doorwings:** Car-Specific Wing-Kibble (seen on mechs such as Prowl)

# **Lower Regions**
**Pelvic Armor:** Pelvis
**Aft:** Buttocks

# **Arms & Legs**
**Servos:** Hands
**Digits:** Fingers
**Pedes:** Feet
**Heel-Struts:** Heel without thrusters
**Thrusters:** Heels (With thrusters in the foot, for example see G1 seekers vs elita-1)
