## Background
Trinemates with [[Brainstorm]] and [[Spacecase]]. Was injured in an accident caused by Brainstorm not long after their onlining. While this accident is what caught the eye of the Decepticon Research Division for Brainstorm, it also meant that Spacecase and Timewise were forced to deal with the fallout of the accident without their trinemate.

Timewise and Brainstorm have an extremely strained relationship. Timewise blames his trinemate for Spacecase’s injuries, and holds a lot of resentment over the fact that the event that injured their third was also the one to put Brainstorm on High Command’s radar.

Both Spacecase and Timewise ended up in Espionage Division of the Decepticon Army, originally to be able to keep in touch with their trinemate as Brainstorm had been stationed on a long-term undercover mission in the Autobot research team. With Brainstorm being the CSO, the two of them are also expected to have on-base duties, rather than in-the-field missions, although Spacecase still does smaller solo missions.
