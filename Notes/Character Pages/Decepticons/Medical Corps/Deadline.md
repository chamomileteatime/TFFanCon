# Background
Forged in Rodion and unfortunately roped into a drug trafficking ring in the Dead End; he was pulled out of an awful situation by [[Viceroy]], a polity senator willing to hire him for his unique medical abilities and secratorial efficiency. Though he worked as a secretary for a while, he loathed it. He loathed [[Golden Hour]] even more. He had no qualms about leaving to join the Decepticons, even despite how overbearing and toxic his relationship with Viceroy ended up being.

He frequently runs into [[Soma]], whom he regards as being boring and complacent. It surprises no one they have a lot in common.
