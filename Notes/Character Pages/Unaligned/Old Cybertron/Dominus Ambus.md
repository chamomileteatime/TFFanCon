## Background
Head (and sole member, for a while) of the House of Ambus. Purchased (and later liberated) [[Rewind]], raised [[Ultra Magnus|Minimus Ambus]], befriended and radicalized [[Shockwave]]. He was an outspoken political activist and philosophical writer assassinated by the Cybertronian government; shortly before, he wrote [[The Ascetic Cybertronian]], which would later inspire [[Megatron]] to write [[Towards Peace]].
