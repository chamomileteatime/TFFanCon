# Background
Part of [[Shockwave]]’s sparklet outlier project, alongside [[Tarn]] and several others. Group up close to Damus, lost track of the other when the war started, potentially still looking for him.

Currently the SpecOps partner to [[Getaway]], both of whom quietly work under [[Prowl]]. Their current mission has them serving the [[Quintesson Hive]] as double agents.

As Skids was infected by the [[Quintesson Control Virus]], he is forced to somewhat frequently use Prowl’s Failsafe, which deletes sections of his memory on-command to keep the Quintessons to be able to control this. Repeated use of this failsafe has however resulted in his memory management systems degrading over time.
