# The Original Thirteen
## Prima
### The First Bearer of the Matrix

The eldest of the Original Thirteen, and the first to bear the Matrix of Leadership. He and Megatronus frequently got in violent arguments about how to properly rule over Cybertron, arguments that only Solus could effectively quell. However, the two eventually went too far, Prima accidentally killing his brother-in-arms. Solus died shortly after, broken by the consequences of their infighting. 

Shortly after this accident, Prima was murdered by Megatronus’ mentee, Liege Maximus, as revenge for the deaths of both Megatronus and Solus Prime. The Matrix of Leadership was then passed on to Prima’s own student, Vector Prime.

## Megatronus Prime
### The Fallen

Megatronus Prime was once the most respected of Prima’s brethren, offering his brother-in-arms advice wherever possible. However, the two had different visions for the future of Cybertron, leading them to fight constantly over their next moves. In one such fight, Prima accidentally killed Megatronus while attempting to defend himself from the other’s attack. Solus died shortly after.

Public perception of Megatronus is very complex, heading into the modern era. While later on the Decepticons would use his likeness and theory as the basis of their own, before this change any sort of reverence towards Megatronus was seen as abnormal; going against the collective mindset of Cybertron.

## Solus Prime
### The First to Love, “Endurus Prime”**

Solus Prime was just as much a core part of Cybertron’s leadership as Prima or Megatronus, both on her own merit and for being the one to keep the other two in check long enough for them to actually lead. However, as Prima’s Amica Endura, and the Conjunx Endura of Megatronus Prime, her “failure” to keep the two in check is used as the basis for pushback against personal relationships across Cybertron. 

Public perception of Solus Prime was, quite frankly, absolutely horrendous. She has been blamed for every problem on Cybertron imagineable, from the deaths of both herself and Megatronus, to modern anti-functionist sentiment. The Femmes, an anti-Functionist counter-culture movement, rose up in the late Golden Era as a way to push back both against the negative perception of Solus Prime, as well as the criminalization of spark bonds.

## Liege Maximus
### “The Betrayer”
Megatronus Prime’s protege. The first to leave Iacon following the deaths of Megatronus and Solus. Said to be a great warrior, even greater than Megatronus. After parting, Liege Maximus went on to lead a tribe called the Maximals, known for their brutality and cunning feats of warfare.

### Appearance, Artifacts, and Abilities


## Vector Prime
### “The Second Bearer of the Matrix” 
Following the death of Prima, the Matrix of Leadership passed on to his student, Vector Prime. Vector was on the younger side of the Thirteen Primes, and often perceived as brash and over-emotional. Despite his attempts to be a good leader to his brethren, many of them left before he had even a first chance to prove himself as a capable leader.

Whether or not Vector had any chance of being a good leader is widely debated, but the truth stands that his failure to restore the bonds between the Primes in his lifetime, regardless of his several attempts to gather those who were left on Cybertron, shows that at the very least he likely tried. His tribe, the original Autobots, would remain the largest subsect of the Cybertronian population, and would eventually become the founders of Iacon.

## Quintessus Prime
### “The First Explorer”
Quintessus was a scientist, and the first of the Thirteen Primes to refuse Vector Prime’s leadership. Taking with him a small retinue of explorers, the prime left to explore the empty space surrounding Cybertron, searching for explanations to their species’ existence. 

Sent back a distress signal regarding a specific alien species. This distress signal was not discovered until the era of Nova Prime, by which point Quintessus and his retinue of scientists had all either been killed or enslaved. The aliens which attacked them would come to be known as the “Quintessons.”

## Onyx Prime
### “Lord of the Beasts”
Onyx Prime was once one of Solus’ dearest friends, even if his streak of jealousy often put him at odds with both Prima and Megatronus. While Onyx was a loner, he felt that Solus understood him in a way no other of his brethren did, and resented Prima for being the one she named her Amica.

Onyx was the second of the remaining Primes to leave the Primal Basilica. A loner not unlike Quintessus, it is unknown if his leaving was due to a lack of respect for Vector, or rather him seeing an opportunity to strike out on his own and taking it. Some claim he simply could not stand to live another millennia in the shadow of Prima, especially with Solus dead. Onyx’s tribe, the Predacons, were said to have once been the most esteemed hunters in all of Cybertron.

### Appearance, Artifacts, and Abilities
Onyx Prime had an appearance unlike any of his brethren. He was extremely tall, shorter only than Nexus Prime, The First Combiner. He body plan was likewise unprecedently unique—with two sets of pedes like a centaur and a large set of wings.

## Amalgamous Prime
Amalgamous was once among the most friendly of the original Thirteen, although his friendliness was of the distant sort. While originally not one to abandon the rest of his brethren, as it became more and more clear that the original Primes were separating for good, he too took his leave of the Primal Basilica, taking with him a group of Cybertronians, taking them to the Acid Wastes which would come later to be parts of Stanix and Kaon. His tribe would later come to be known as the Junkions, for their unique make up and lifestyle.

## Micronus Prime
### “Smallest of the Thirteen”
Micronus Prime had once been the moral core of the Thirteen, before their dissolution. Despite his small stature Micronus was patient and wise, often offering the most modest solution to any issue the Thirteen faced. Like Alpha Trion, Micronus did not leave the Primal Basilica in the traditional way, rather carving out a place for him and his tribesmen not far from where his brethren had once ruled together, making them one of the three founding tribes of Iacon.

Micronus’ tribe would come to be known as the Minicons, as those of the smallest weight classes of Cybertronians disproportionately owed their allegiance to the smallest Prime. Micronus had promised them a home away from the Primal Basilica, one where their size was the norm, not the exception. As one of the core three tribes credited with the creation of Iacon, Micronus’ promise remains the reason that smaller weight classes remain more accommodated than larger ones well into the present day.

## Alpha Trion
### First of the Archives
Unlike many of his brethren, Alpha Trion remained in Iacon alongside Prima, although he did retain his own faction, the Archivists. The Archivists were a group that dedicated themselves to recording all of Cybertron’s history, and uniquely amongst the tribes of the Thirteen lasted well into the Golden Age of Cybertron. Because of this, the Archivist Tribe had a abnormal spin on Functionism and the Functionist Caste system, and often attempted to separate themselves from broader Cybertronian culture as much as possible. Members of the Archivist Tribe still alive today include Optimus Prime, a former cargo technician for the tribe, and Rewind who was a Monitor for the Hall of Records before being taken and enslaved as a disposable.

## Meliorous Prime
### “The Gentle One”
It is said that Meliorous Prime was a seer, as well as the one to find the Matrix of Leadership within the first hotspot (rumored to be Alyon in Nyon).

Very little is known of Meliorous’ personality, beyond writings left by some of the Thirteen describing him as an idealist and of gentle spark, as well as several “prophecies” said to be written by them. Many credit him as the inventor of the mantra, “Til All Are One.”

Melirious never went on to found a Tribe of their own, with some early sources suggesting that they might have instead disappeared into the wilderness of early Cybertron to live amongst the mechanimals. Some odd theories suggest that Meliorous still lives, with “sightings” being common in distant regions. 

His title, “The Best of Us All,” comes from a letter written by Prima to Alpha Trion shortly after the Thirteen went their separate ways. Meliorous was the one to discover the Matrix of Leadership, supposedly because he was the only one Primus could trust not to hide the artifact away for himself. Likewise, when the eleven remaining members of the Thirteen splintered the Cybertronian population into groups, Meliorous refused to do so as well, instead leaving the Primal Basilica alone.

Meliorous is believed to be the protector of pacifists and non-combatants. Modernly, the image of Meliorous leaving the Basilica alone is considered especially poignant, and his code is most discussed in the context of the splintering of the Cybertronian peoples into two factions. 

Their symbol, Meliorus’ Cross, is cross shape splint into four odd-shaped boxes. Supposedly represents how the unity of Cybertron has been split across many factions and city-states. It is traditionally illegal for use outside of specifically approved medical personal and aid workers. Doubles as a sign of non-combatance. Attacking someone displaying Meliorous’ Cross on their plating is traditionally considered a rather grievous crime, although the Autobot-Decepticon Civil War has cooled this taboo significantly.
