# The Original Thirteen
## The First — Prima
**The First Bearer of the Matrix**

Those who take after Prima in terms of personality tend to be strong, intuition-led leaders. Their failings, however, are often deeply tied to this sense of leadership—they often force themselves to take on burdens they cannot manage alone, or act in a way that limits harm to those under their protection, rather than splitting their burdens equally or trusting others. They also often react poorly to criticism, and are prone to doubling down and attempt to prove others wrong when their way is not working.

## The Second — Megatronus Prime
**The Fallen**

He and Prima got into a fight over something, which resulted in Prima accidentally killing him. The death of his spark also killed his Conjunx Endura, Solus Prime, resulting in great tragedy for the Thirteen, leaving them all to go their separate ways.

**The First to Love, “Endurus Prime”**

As Prima’s Amica Endura, and the Conjunx Endura of Megatronus Prime, her “failure to keep the two in check” is used as the basis for pushback against personal relationships across Cybertron.

## The Twelfth — Alpha Trion
**First of the Archivists**

Unlike many of his brethren, Alpha Trion remained in Iacon alongside Prima, although he did retain his own faction, the Archivists. The Archivists were a group that dedicated themselves to recording all of Cybertron’s history, and uniquely amongst the tribes of the Thirteen lasted well into the Golden Age of Cybertron. Because of this, the Archivist Tribe had a abnormal spin on Functionism and the Functionist Caste system, and often attempted to separate themselves from broader Cybertronian culture as much as possible. Members of the Archivist Tribe still alive today include Optimus Prime, a former cargo technician for the tribe, and Rewind who was a Monitor for the Hall of Records before being taken and enslaved as a disposable.

## The Thirteenth — Meliorous Prime
**The Best of Us All**

It is said that Meliorous Prime was a seer, as well as the one to find the Matrix of Leadership within the first hotspot (rumored to be Alyon in Nyon).

Very little is known of Meliorous’ personality, beyond writings left by some of the Thirteen describing him as an idealist and of gentle spark, as well as several “prophecies” said to be written by them. Many credit him as the inventor of the mantra, “Til All Are One.”

Melirious never went on to found a Tribe of their own, with some early sources suggesting that they might have instead disappeared into the wilderness of early Cybertron to live amongst the mechanimals. Some odd theories suggest that Meliorous still lives, with “sightings” being common in distant regions. 

His title, “The Best of Us All,” comes from a letter written by Prima to Alpha Trion shortly after the Thirteen went their separate ways. Meliorous was the one to discover the Matrix of Leadership, supposedly because he was the only one Primus could trust not to hide the artifact away for himself. Likewise, when the eleven remaining members of the Thirteen splintered the Cybertronian population into groups, Meliorous refused to do so as well, instead leaving the Primal Basilica alone.

Meliorous is believed to be the protector of pacifists and non-combatants. Modernly, the image of Meliorous leaving the Basilica alone is considered especially poignant, and his code is most discussed in the context of the splintering of the Cybertronian peoples into two factions. 

Their symbol, Meliorus’ Cross, is cross shape splint into four odd-shaped boxes. Supposedly represents how the unity of Cybertron has been split across many factions and city-states. It is traditionally illegal for use outside of specifically approved medical personal and aid workers. Doubles as a sign of non-combatance. Attacking someone displaying Meliorous’ Cross on their plating is traditionally considered a rather grievous crime, although the Autobot-Decepticon Civil War has cooled this taboo significantly.
