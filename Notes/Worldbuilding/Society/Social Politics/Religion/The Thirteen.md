## The Thirteenth — Meliorous Prime
**The Best of Us All**
### Background
*Altmode:* Unknown

It is said that Meliorous Prime was a seer, as well as the one to find the Matrix of Leadership within the first hotspot (rumored to be Alyon in Nyon).

Very little is known of Meliorous’ personality, beyond writings left by some of the Thirteen describing him as an idealist and of gentle spark, as well as several “prophecies” said to be written by them. Many credit him as the inventor of the mantra, “Til All Are One.”

Melirious never went on to found a Tribe of their own, with some early sources suggesting that they might have instead disappeared into the wilderness of early Cybertron to live amongst the mechanimals. Some odd theories suggest that Meliorous still lives, with “sightings” being common in distant regions. 

His title, “The Best of Us All,” comes from a letter written by Prima to Alpha Trion shortly after the Thirteen went their separate ways. Meliorous was the one to discover the Matrix of Leadership, supposedly because he was the only one Primus could trust not to hide the artifact away for himself. Likewise, when the eleven remaining members of the Thirteen splintered the Cybertronian population into groups, Meliorous refused to do so as well, instead leaving the Primal Basilica alone.

### Religion
**Patron:** Medics
Meliorous’ Cult is associated with many concepts. They are believed to be the protector of pacifists and non-combatants. Modernly, the image of Meliorous leaving the Basilica alone is considered especially poignant, and his code is most discussed in the context of the splintering of the Cybertronian peoples into two factions. 

#### Meliorous’ Cross — ![[SYMBOL - Meliorous' Cross.png]]
A red cross shape splint into four odd-shaped boxes. Supposedly represents how the unity of Cybertron has been split across many factions and city-states. 

Illegal for use outside of specifically approved medical personal and aid workers. Doubles as a sign of non-combatance. Attacking someone displaying Meliorous’ Cross on their plating is traditionally considered a rather grievous crime, although the Autobot-Decepticon Civil War has cooled this taboo significantly.
### Inheritance
**Western Zodiac Equivalent:** N/A, Ophiuchus
**Boons:**  Idealistic & Kind-Hearted
**Bane:** Non-Conformist
