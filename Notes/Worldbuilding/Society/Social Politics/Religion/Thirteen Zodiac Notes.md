# The Zodiac of the Thirteen
## Important Terms
### Three Functions
Most Cybertronians split up their “inheritance” from the Thirteen Primes into three functions: their brain module, their spark, and their transformation-cog.
#### Code Lineage — Goals & Faults
One’s Code Lineage shows how they speak to themselves internally and in private. It reveals both their goals as well as their deepest faults and struggles.
#### Spark Lineage — Convictions & Fears
One’s Spark Lineage shows how they reacted to situations emotionally. While the Code Lineage reflects what they tell themselves, the Spark Lineage reveals their innermost convictions as well as their fears, often hidden to both themselves and others.
#### Morph Lineage — Presence & Blind Spot
One’s Morph Lineage does not reflect themselves, but rather how they are perceived by those around them. This reflects the behaviors obvious to those around them, but that might be hidden from oneself. Rather than displaying a negative trait of themselves, the secondary characteristic of one’s Morph Lineage instead shows their blind spot within their one personality—the parts of themself that they are the least aware of.
#### Aggressor & Ruler
When discussing interactions between functions, when the functions are not the same (ie, Spark to Spark), the interaction gains a “ruler.” This ruler refers to which of the two functions (or which party, in the case of interpersonal interactions) are the one most affected by the interaction. The aggressor likewise refers to the party that incites the strong reaction within the ruler. 

A way to remember these relationships is the follow orders: 

<u>Solo:</u>
Code = Spark > Morph
	Spark and Code conflict with one another, while both are aggressed by the blind spot created by the Morph Lineage.

<u> Interpersonal: </u>
Spark > Code > Morph
	The Spark party is aggressed by both the Code and the Morph, while the Code aggresses the Spark and is aggressed by the Morph.

## Interactions
### Solo
#### Code Vs Spark
**Ruler:** N/A
**Aggressor:** Both

The difference between the Code and the Spark reflects the core conflict present within one’s internal monologue. The Code often disguises the Spark, attempting to make sense of and warp the Spark’s fears and convictions to match it’s own Goals. Likewise, the Spark struggles against the faults of the Code, often feeling a deep anguish at how it raises the chances of its fears occurring. f these two factors are too similar, it can create a “Double Negative,” in which one’s fears are constantly being activated by their faults, often leading to a spiraling personality.
#### Code Vs Morph
**Ruler:** Code
**Aggressor:** Morph

The blind spot of the Morph serves as a source of power to the faults of the Code. If these two factors are too similar, it can create a “Blind Fault,” where one is incapable of recognizing the negative sides of their own personality.
#### Spark vs Morph
### Interpersonal
#### Code to Code
**Look To:** C+/C- = High

Code to Code matchups have the benefit of often having smooth interactions with one another, with the downside of doubling down on the faults that both Cybertronians have. Instead, compatible Code matchups are often different enough to not have overlapping faults, but not so different as to create opposing faults instead.
#### Code to Spark
**Look To:** C-/S+ Overlap = Low
**Ruler:** The Spark
**Aggressor:** The Code

Code to Spark incompatibility often serve as the core cause of mental unrest within a sole Cybertronian, but this role is taken by Code to Morph relations in interpersonal relationships. Instead, a Code to Spark relationship reflects how one reacts to the faults of the other.

#### Code to Morph
**Look To:** M+/C+ = High & M+/C- = Low
**Ruler:** The Code
**Aggressor:** The Morph

Code to Morph interactions are the most essential two understanding the compatibility between two people. This is because the Code Lineage represents one’s faults in their way of thinking, which applies not only to themselves but to those around them. An incompatible Code to Morph matchup often results in the Code-side unduly judging the Morph-side of the relationship, and often holding them accountable.
#### Spark to Spark
**Look To:** S-/S- = High
Spark to Spark matchups are do not have a strong effect on interaction and compatibility, as they represent the repressed and hidden sides of both parties. Instead, matching Spark to Spark matchups give one the benefit of seeing their struggles from on outside perspective, and can often lead both parties to gain a greater deal of understanding about their own inner demons. Incompatible Spark signs often result in both parties perceiving a subconscious feeling that something is wrong with the other.
#### Spark to Morph
**Look To:** S-/M+ = High
**Ruler:** The Spark
**Aggressor:** The Morph

Cybertronians often subconsciously seek out those with Morph Lineages matching to their own Spark Lineage. This is because the Morph Lineage represents how one might see the other, and on a surface level the other appears as someone understanding of their deepest fears. An incompatible Spark to Morph matchup often results in the Spark-side unduly judging the Morph-side of the relationship, and often holding them accountable for their own faults.
#### Morph to Morph
**Look To:** N/A

Morph to Morph interactions are the least relevant to compatibility, as they primarily focus on the outer appearance of the two Cybertronians involved.

## Features & Dialogues
### Terms
#### Primary - External Vs. Internal
Does it center the self? Or others?
#### Secondary - Extroverted Vs. Introverted
Is it given by someone else? Or can it be achieved alone?
#### Tertiary - Egocentric Vs. Altruistic
Does it benefit the self? Or others?
#### Fixation
A fixation is a feature which oscillates between the two states. This masks all other dialogues, and takes control over the feature.
#### Null
A null is a dialogue which has no effect on the broader self at all. It most often occurs when a dialogue experiences a fixation, dulling the effects other features. It also occurs rarely in the triple null trait, which experiences total apathy towards that feature.
### Attention (AT)
**Dialogue:** External/Extroverted/Egocentric, “I center myself by asking others to give me what I need.”

**Positive Feature:** Validation/Validation-Seeking, “I need your attention.”

**Negative Feature:** Ridicule/Self-Conscious, “I don’t want any attention.”

### Narcissism (NR)
**Dialogue:** Internal/Extroverted/Egocentric, “I center others asking them to give me what I need.”

**Positive Feature:** Ambition/Ambitious, “I want you to give me what I need.”

**Negative Feature:** Dependent, “I don’t want my own needs.”

### Privacy (PR)
**Dialogue:** External/Introverted/Egocentric, “I center others by giving myself what I need.”

**Positive Feature:** Isolation/Confident, “I need to be alone to be helpful.”

**Negative Feature:** Sycophantic, “I don’t want to be alone, I want to be helpful.”

### Reflection (RF)
**Dialogue:** Internal/Introverted/Egocentric, “I center myself by giving me what I need.”

**Positive Feature:** Introspection/Proactive,  “I want to reflect.”

**Negative Feature:** Negligent, “There is nothing to reflect.”

### Detachment (DT)
**Dialogue:** External/Extroverted/Altruistic, “I center others by asking them to do what benefits them.”

**Positive Feature:** Caution/Cautious, “I want to be left alone.”

**Negative Feature:** Abandoned, “I don’t want to be left alone.” 

### Misery (MI)
**Dialogue:** Internal/Extroverted/Altruistic, “I center myself in letting others do what benefits them.”

**Positive Feature:** Masochism/Masochistic, “I want you to hurt me.”

**Negative Feature:** Paranoia/Paranoid, “I don’t want you to hurt me.”

### Compassion - (CM)
**Dialogue:** External/Introverted/Altruistic, “I center others by doing everything to help them.”

**Positive Feature:** Empathy/Empathetic, “I want to help you.”

**Negative Feature:** Pessimism, “No one does anything to help anyone.”

### Contribution - (CT)
**Dialogue:** Internal/Introverted/Altruistic, “I center myself in doing things that helps others.”

**Positive Feature:** Philanthropy/Philanthropic, “I want to see myself help you.”

**Negative Feature:** Megalomaniacal, “I don’t want to see myself helping anyone.”

### Publicity - (PB)
**Dialogue:** Fix/Null/Null, “Do I center myself, or do I center others?”

**Positive Feature:** Attraction/Attractive, “I want everyone to center me!”

**Negative Feature:** Reclusive/Reclusion, “I don’t want to center anyone, myself or others.”

### Connection - (CN)
**Dialogue:** Null/Fix/Null, “Do I rely on others, or do I rely on myself?”

**Positive Feature:** Proximity/Proximity-seeking, “I want us all to rely on each other.”

**Negative Feature:** Recusant, “I want none of us to rely on each other.”

### Ethics - (ET)
**Dialogue:** Null/Null/Fix, “Am I egocentric, or am I altruistic?”

**Positive Feature:** Morality/Moral, “I want to always choose the right thing.”

**Negative Feature:** Shameful, “I fear I never choose to do the right thing.”

### Freedom - (FR)
**Dialogue:** Fix/Fix/Fix, “I don’t know what I am.”

**Positive Feature:** Freedom/Free-Spirited, “I am okay with not knowing what to do.”

**Negative Feature:** Insecure, “I don’t want to not know what to do.”

### Apathy - (AP)
**Dialogue:** Null/Null/Null, “I don’t care what I am.”

**Positive Feature:** Peace/Ambivalent, “I don’t care.”

**Negative Feature:** Obsessive, “I don’t want to not care.”

## Code Lineages
### Prima (CPri)
(CT+ AT-), “The Leader”
**Goal:** Philanthropy (CN+), “My goal is to help these people.”
**Fault:** Self-Conscious (AT-), “I struggle to face negative attention.”

CPri represents the archetype of “The Leader.” They want to help people through control, offering them guidance before taking over the situation if they do not follow it. This desire for control also leads them to struggle with criticism, as it challenges not only their view of the world, but what they believe their value to others to be.

### Megatronus Prime (CMga)
(MI+ CT-), “The Insurgent”
**Goal:** Masochism (MI+), “My goal is to make you hurt me.”
**Fault:** Megalomaniacal (CT-), “I struggle to honestly help those around me.”

CMga represents the archetype of the “Insurgent.” They often have an unclouded and perceptive view of the world around them. But rather than operate on their own, they often try to force others into seeing the world the way they do. They believe in a might-makes-right mentality, and will goad others into arguments and fights in an attempt to communicate with them. Because of this, they struggle to help those around them outside of their violent reactions, and often need someone around them able to lead them away from bad decisions to operate normally.

### Solus Prime (CSol)
(CN+ PR-), “The Navigator”
**Goal:** Proximity (CN+), “My goal is to keep the peace.”
**Fault:** Sycophantic (PR-), “I often struggle being alone, I need to be helpful.”

CSol represents the archetype of “The Navigator.” They often surround themselves with peers they feel a responsibility over, and view each and every disagreement as their issue to solve. They find themselves without a calling when left alone, and therefore will do anything and everything they can think of to keep their peers in close proximity, regardless of others’ wants or need.

### Quintessus Prime (CQnt)
(PR+ NR-), “The Wanderer”
**Goal:** Isolation (PR+), “My goal is to helpful in being alone.”
**Fault:** Dependent (NR-), “I struggle to create my own wants.”

CQnt represents the archetype of “The Wanderer.” They feel that they work best alone, and struggle to achieve their function when forced in close proximity with others. At the same time, they often consider themselves to operate more as a machine than a person—forgetting they have their own needs and desires and isolating themselves completely.

### Liege Maximus (CLie)
(NR+ DT-), “The King”
**Goal:** Ambition (NR+), “My goal is to be better than you.” 
**Fault:** Abandoned (DT-), “I struggle when I am left alone.”

CLie represents the archetype of “The King.” They want to be considered a rank above all of their peers, but at the same time struggle in the face of isolation. They are often brash and abrasive, but quick to take a step back when they feel they have crossed a line, even if they often struggle to apologize for it with words. 

### Alchemius Prime (CAlc)
(CM+ MI-), “The Angel”
**Goal:** Empathy (CM+), “My goal is to help you.”
**Fault:** Paranoia (MI-), “I struggle for a long time if you hurt me.”

CAlc represents the archetype of “The Angel.” They derive great joy from helping others, but any slight against them is taken as a sign to never offer their assistance again. They are deeply loyal, and no amount of flattery can ever hope to recover the slight they feel they received. Despite this, actually pushing them beyond that line is often surprisingly difficult, and the gifts they bring are well-worth a little bit of sucking up.

### Micronus Prime (CMcr)
(ET+ FR-), “The Mediator”
**Goal:** Morality (ET+), “My goal is to always choose the right thing.”
**Fault:** Insecure (FR-), “I struggle to feel I know what to do.”

CMcr represents the archetype of “The Mediator.” They want to do the right thing for everyone involved, often attempting to find a middle ground between their own desires and those of others. Because of this draw to debate, they often find themselves frequently participating in relationships of give and take. Their fault is that they rely heavily on this sense of morality as a double-fix trait, and struggle to navigate situations where debate is not an option.

### Onyx Prime (COnx)
(AP+ CM-), “The Hermit”
**Goal:** Peace (AP+), “My goal is for nothing to happen.”
**Fault:** Pessimism (CM-), “I struggle to help anyone.”

CAmg represents the archetype of “The Hermit.” They often have a negative perception of the world, wanting to be left alone. Their most notable trait is often how they lie both to themself and others about their goals and thoughts, and fail to realize what they want in the face of giving in to the desires of others. Likewise, they judge those who center themselves, be it through selfish means or by having extravagant goals or codes of honor.

### Amalgamous Prime (CAmg)
(DT+ CN-), “The Listener”
**Goal:** Caution (DT+), “My goal is to be left alone.”
**Fault:** Recusant (CN-), “I struggle to not judge people helping each other.”

CAmg represents the archetype of “The Listener.” They center others in every way, and therefore struggle to understand their own needs. They feel that the altruistic lifestyle is to exist as a person only in private, and judge those who wear their heart on their sleeve, and those who support them,

### Vector Prime (CVcr)
(AT+ ET-), “The Mutineer”
**Goal:** Validation (AT+), “My goal is for everyone to notice me.”
**Fault:** Shameful (ET-), “I struggle to know if I am doing the right thing.”

CVcr represents the archetype of “The Mutineer.” They desire validation for their actions over everything, and are willing to go a long way to achieve it. Their fault is their shame, as often feel as this desire for validation is selfish, and instead often find themselves in positions of power or leadership in order to draw this 

### Nexus Prime (CNxs)
(PB+ PR-), “The Harlequin”
**Goal:** Attraction (PB+), “My goal is for everyone to see me.”
**Fault:** Negligent (FR-), “I struggle to reflect on my behavior”

CNxs represents the archetype of “The Innocent.” Their internal monologue is often very quiet, and instead they direct this focus completely on the world around them. As a consequence, CNxs often struggles to comprehend the inner world of others, and fails to understand why they do not receive the same amount of attention which they devote to others. Additionally, this lack of an inner world reflects itself in how they struggle to reflect on their own behavior, for better or for worse.

### Alpha Trion (CAtr)
(FR+ PB-), “The Neutral”
**Goal:** Freedom (FR+), “My goal is to no longer be needed.”
**Fault:** Reclusive (PB-), “I struggle to center myself or others.”

CAtr represents the archetype of “The Neutral.” They feel that they owe this neutrality to the world around them, but deeply desire the day that they finally feel free to no longer be necessary, despite this burden existing entirely in their own head. The neutrality of CAtr also means that they struggle to center anyone, often attempting to solve each issue from an entirely neutral and logical angle. 

### Meliorous Prime (CMli)
(RF+ AP-), “The Adherent”
**Goal:** Introspection (RF+), “My goal is to get better.”
**Fault:** Obsessive (AP-), “I struggle with the fear that I don’t care enough.”

CMli represents the archetype of “The Adherent.” They want to do everything they can to help people, not realizing that their concept of “help” is so focused on internal betterment that they often fail to make real change in the outside world. They are often constantly checking their own internal dialogue against their strict moral code, and holding themselves to a standard of moral purity that is nearly impossible to reach. They often find themselves going groups with clear goals and/or strict moral codes which they can hold themselves to.

## Spark Lineages
### Prima (SPri)
(AT+ AP-), “The Fawn”
**Conviction:** Validation (AT+), “I believe goodness comes from others telling me I am good.”
**Fear:** Obsessive (AP-), “What if I stop caring?”

SPri represents the archetype of “The Fawn.” They follow the words of others—believing that they are good only when they are told they are good. Their greatest fear, however, is the looming danger of their own apathy. If they stop trusting the words of others, how will they possibly continue on?

### Megatronus Prime (SMga)
(CN+ NR-), “The Warden”
**Conviction:** (CN+) “I believe goodness comes from us all helping others.”
**Fear:** Dependence (NR-), “What if I am being selfish?”

SMga represents the archetype of “The Warden.” They believe that goodness can be present in all people—but only if they are serving the needs of others. Their greatest fear is that the help they work so hard to give—and convince others to give, comes from a selfish desire within them, rather than true altruism. The concept that they are not operating from true goodness and moral purity deeply concerns them, as does the sight of those incapable of helping others because they need help themselves.

### Solus Prime (SSol)
(CT+ ET-), “The Caretaker”
**Conviction:** Charity (CT+), “I believe goodness comes when others view me as good for helping them.”
**Fear:** Shame (ET-), “What if I’m not doing the right thing?”

SSol represents the archetype of “The Caretaker.” They are deeply tied to a small group of peers, and believe that their value comes from the praise and thanks they are given for taking care of this group. Their deepest fear is their shame—they are so focused on providing for those close to them, that they often worry that this assistance they provide might actually be a harm and not help.

### Quintessus Prime (SQnt)
(DT+ FR-), “The Vagabond”
**Conviction:** Caution (DT+), “I believe goodness comes only when we all leave each other alone.”
**Fear:** Insecure (FR-), “I fear not knowing what I am.”

SQnt represents the archetype of “The Vagabond.” They push away their emotional core, instead believing that solitude and introspection can be used to control their feelings and keep them in line. Ironically, their greatest fear is their perceived lack of identity, caused by their refusal of their own emotions.

### Liege Maximus (SLie)
(PB+ MI-), “The Neonate”
**Conviction:** Attraction (PB+), “I believe goodness comes from being valued.”
**Fear:** Paranoia (MI-), “I fear getting hurt.” 

SLie represents the archetype of “The Neonate.” They value their inner world being understood by others, and need their perspective and life story to be taken seriously. The worst thing one can do to a SLie is pretend that their life has had little meaning so far. The greatest fear of SLie is their own base instincts, rather than any more abstract concept. They fear getting hurt, they fear being unsafe, and the fear loosing access to resources. In many ways, SLie is very simplistic, but at the same time this allows them a deep level of connection to their emotions.

### Alchemius Prime (SAlc)
(FR+ CT-), “The Spirit”
**Conviction:** Free-Spirited (FR+), “I believe goodness comes from giving others the choice to do anything.”
**Fear:** Megalomania (CT-), “I fear burning out on helping other people.”

SAlc represents the archetype of “The Spirit.” They believe that they can best help others by given them a safe space to do whatever it is they need. Likewise, they fear tiring themselves out, and loosing the ability to go with the flow of what people need in the moment. Like many others they struggle to put their own needs and emotions first, but unlike others they are often aware that they are doing it.

### Micronus Prime (SMcr)
(RF+ AT-), “The Changeling”
**Conviction:** Improvement (RF+), “I believe goodness comes from reflection.”
**Fear:** Ridicule (AT-), “I fear being made fun of for who I am.”

SMcr represents the archetype of “The Changeling.” They believe that to be good is to change whatever about you is bad. As a result, they are constantly attempting to better themselves to only feel emotions that fit with their moral code. They deeply fear falling behind in this effort, and being perceived by others to be wrong in any way.

### Onyx Prime (SOnx)
(MI+ CN-), “The Self-Flagellant”
**Conviction:** Masochism (MI+), “I belive goodness comes from being hurt.”
**Fear:** Recuse (CN-), “I fear being removed from the conversation.”

SOnx represents the archetype of “The Self-Flagellant.” They believe that the world is out to hurt them specifically, and believe that their goodness comes from their oppression by those around them. The greatest fear of SOnx, therefore is their view of the world being cast aside by others, and the potential that their suffering means nothing in the grand scheme of the world.

### Amalgamous Prime (SAmg)
(CM+ PB-), “The Succubus”
**Conviction:** Empathy (CM+), “I believe goodness comes in the form of helping others” 
**Fear:** Reclusion (PB-), “My fear is you seeing me and not liking it.”

SAmg represents the archetype of “The Succubus.” Their view of themselves and what they want others to see are extremely different. They often put forward the face of a empathetic soul, always helpful and understanding. However, they do this to disguise what they believe is a horrible reality underneath. This “hidden self” is the greatest fear of SAmg, and they will go to great lengths to hide it from anyone they meet.

### Vector Prime (SVcr)
(NR+ PR-) “The Paragon”
**Conviction:** Ambition (NR+), “I believe goodness comes from being the best.”
**Fear:** Sycophancy (PR-), “I fear isolation.”

SVcr represents the archetype of “The Paragon.” The derive great joy from being the best at something, and will take any and every chance to prove themselves better than their peers in the skills they believe hold value. Conversely, their greatest fear is isolation, or existing in a space where there is no one to compare themselves to. In extreme cases an SVcr might even “dumb down” their skill in an effort to keep their peers engaged in the competition.

### Nexus Prime (SNxs)
(AP+ DT-), “The Jester”
**Conviction:** Ambivalence (AP+), “I believe goodness comes from not caring too much.”
**Fear:** Abandonment (DT-), “I fear being abandoned.”

SNxs represents the archetype of “The Jester.” They believe that a good person doesn’t care too much about what’s going on, and is instead focused on everyone having a good time. This is because they fear nothing more than their peers abandoning them for disagreeing. Often very positive and laid-back, and very prone to falling in with bad crowds.

### Alpha Trion (SAtr)
(ET+ RF-), “The Judge”
**Conviction:** Morality (ET+), “I believe goodness comes from being proven right.”
**Fear:** Negligence (RF-), “I fear being caught unaware.”

SAtr represents the archetype of “The Judge.” They chase being proven right above all other virtues, and often struggle to see other sides in issues that are not so black and white. Their greatest fear is being caught unaware, and having to face the realization that there is something they don’t know thus proving that they are not always correct.

### Meliorous Prime (SMli)
(PR+ CM-), “The Soothsayer”
**Conviction:** Isolation (PR+), “I believe goodness comes from having space to think.”
**Fear:** Pessimism (CM-), “I fear one day believing that no one helps each other.”

SMli represents the archetype of “The Soothsayer.” They believe that they do good by giving others the chance to reflect on what they consider to be bad decisions. They are very prone to handing out criticism, or tossing away peers who refuse to change their mind. There greatest fear is losing their optimistic outlook on life—that one day they might decide that there are some people who can never change.

## Morph Lineages
### Prima (MPri)
(PB+ CM), “The Prisoner”
**Presence:** Attractive (PB+), “I need to present myself in a way people love.”
**Blind Spot:** Compassion (CM), “I fail to understand that people like me.”

MPri represents the archetype of “The Prisoner.” They desperately want to be seen as someone others can love and appreciate, often being willing to cut away parts of their identity to better fit a mold. Their blind spot is Compassion, and recognizing that there are people out there who are like them and will value them as they are.

### Megatronus Prime (MMga)
(RF+ PR), “The Architect”
**Presence:** Introspection (RF+), “I need to present myself as someone striving for a better world.”
**Blind Spot:** Privacy (PR), “I fail to recognize my own emotions in a vacuum.”

MMga represents the archetype of “The Architect.” They want more than anything to be seen as the first person to go to on how to improve the lives of people everywhere. They try to present themselves as many good characteristics: kind, well-read, and even might try to lie about who they are or where they came from. Their blindspot is Privacy, and the idea that it is okay for them to openly be who they are in private, or even that they do not need to fix the world by themself.

### Solus Prime (MSol)
(CM+ FR), “The Laborer”
**Presence:** Empathetic (CM+), “I must present myself as someone who can always help.”
**Blind Spot:** Freedom (FR), “I fail to recognize that I am free to do as I please.”

MSol represents the archetype of “The Laborer.” They want to be seen as someone who helps others, often going so far as to make themselves as available as possible for anyone who might need them. The consequence of this is that their blind spot is their own freedom—so focused on making time for others that they fail to realize that some of this time could belong to themselves as well.

### Quintessus Prime (MQnt)
(AP+ AT), “The Drifter”
**Presence:** Ambivalent (AP+), “I will present myself as someone who does not care.”
**Blind Spot:** Attention (AT), “I fail to recognize that people see me and what I do.”

MQnt represents the archetype of “The Drifter.” They like to present themselves as someone who does not care about anything that happens and whether or not it benefits them. They often give people the answers they think they want to hear, and move on with their day. As a consequence, they fail to recognize that this outer persona which they give to others has a real affect on their lives and perceptions of them. A MQnt can go months talking to someone and then be surprised to find out the other actually likes them and thinks about them when they aren’t around.

### Liege Maximus (MLie)
(NR+ RF), “The Divine”
**Presence:** Ambitious (NR+), “I need to present myself as someone who should always come first.”
**Blind Spot:** Reflection (RF), “I fail to see that I do not reflect, nor do I realize that other people are capable of reflection.”

MLie represents the archetype of “The Divine.” The present themselves as the most important person in any room, and often grossly exaggerate their own achievements. They believe everyone—themselves included—to be static entities incapable of change. They do not stop to consider if the person who hurt them years ago has improved and changed, nor do they believe that apologies are guaranteed to mean that someone is sorry. As a consequence, however, they also hold themselves to this same standard, and do not believe themselves as capable of making up for past mistakes either.

### Alchemius Prime (MAlc)
(CT+ CN), “The Demon”
**Presence:** Philanthropist (CT+) "I am supposed to present myself as someone who helps other begrudgingly."
**Blind Spot:** Connection (CN), “I fail to realize I am wanted.”

MAlc represents the archetype of “The Demon.” They try to protect themselves by creating the vision of someone who does not want to help others while not actually being against helping. They often agree to do something only when begged to do so, and often love to complain about the task while doing it, especially if they don’t actually mind it. Their greatest blindspot is that they fail to realize that there are people in their lives that do in fact care about them and want to help them, and that they don’t actually have to create the defense of not wanting to help to not be taken advantage of or seen as soft.

### Micronus Prime (MMcr)
(ET+ AP), “The Arbiter”
**Presence:** Moral (ET+), “I will present myself as someone who values morality about all else.”
**Blind Spot:** Apathy (AP), “I fail to understand that it is okay to not care.”

MMcr represents the archetype of “The Arbiter.” They want to be viewed as impartial, while also failing to understand that it is okay to not have an opinion on every issue brought to them. They are often valued by others for giving good judgement and feedback without being unreasonable. They are however often prone to overworking themselves, and struggle to find hobbies outside of their function.

### Onyx Prime (MOnx)
(DT+ ET), “The Loner”
**Presence:** Cautious (DT+), “I will present myself as someone who does not need other people.”
**Blind Spot:** Ethics (ET), “I fail to consider if something might be egocentric or altruistic.”

MOnx represents the archetype of “The Loner.” They want to be respected as an individual above everything else, and will go great lengths to scare off anyone they feel is attempting to control them in any way. Perhaps fortunately for them, the rarely consider how their actions affect those around them, leading others to give them a wide berth.

### Amalgamous Prime (MAmg)
(CN+ MI), “The Daredevil”
**Presence:** Proximity-Seeking (CN+), "I need to present myself as a team player who is friends with everyone" 
**Blind Spot:** Misery (MI), “I fail to see how things hurt me.”

MAmg represents the archetype of “The Daredevil.” They want to be seen as a team-player, someone who is easy to get along with and to like.
