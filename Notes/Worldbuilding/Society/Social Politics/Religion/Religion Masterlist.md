# Cybertronian Religions
## Religion on Cybertron
While Cybertron is *technically* religious, the average Cybertronian is only expected to be religious in so far as much to agree with what the Functionist Council says. Anything more than that, such as actually reading religious scriptures, is considered abnormal and “too involved.”

## Spectralism
**Type:** Animist
Spectralism is a Cybertronian religious movement focused heavily on the divine as they present themselves within the material world. While it does technically conflict with Neoprimalism across several teachings, it is not uncommon for religious Cybertronians to be both Spectralists *and* Neoprimalists, although it does require some willful ignorance.

Spectralism is, generally, not well received on Cybertron at all. Being too openly Spectralist (especially when it comes to the beliefs systems such as “everyone is equal”) can be grounds for removal from one’s position on Cybertron, and even empurata in very extreme cases.

### Core Beliefs
Spectralists believe that the divine presents itself in the material realm in the form of the Electromagnetic Spectrum, and that Cybertronians are uniquely gifted as beings with Sparks made of pure light and strong Electromagnetic Fields. It is important to note that despite this, a true Spectralist believes that *all* beings and objects are innately divine and spiritually powerful in some way, just that Cybertronians have the gift to be able to see it.
#### Slivers
Slivers are the closest thing to a divine being present in Spectralism. They are beings made of pure light, incomprehensible on a material scale. Unlike the traditional Neoprimalist deity, Slivers do not necessarily have a set consciousness or any sort of authority over other living beings, but rather are frequently treated as unconscious forces of nature, given power and existence through worship and thought. Similar to the concept of an egregore.

Slivers can be related to specific people, or concepts, or anything else that is given power by those around it.

### In Combination with NeoPrimalism
As a sort of “fad” religion, some Cybertronians believe in a mix of NeoPrimalism and Spectralism. Because this is in no way an organized religion, beliefs sway depending on the Cybertronian in question. The most common basis posits that Primus serves as the Sliver tied to the combined unconscious of all Cybertronians, as well as the planet itself. It is through Cybertron’s belief in Primus that this Sliver gains its power, which it then distributes back out amongst its people. 

Adaptus and Mortilus are much less frequent figures in these sorts of beliefs, although several Cybertronians have been detained for the belief that Adaptus is, and I quote, “in reality a Sliver created and fed by the Functionist Council in order to keep Cybertronians from realizing their innate divinity.” Conspiracy Theorists exist in every culture.
