# Signs of Affinity
The Signs of Affinity are a Cybertronian pseudoscience, used to test the theoretical affinity one might have to the Matrix of Leadership. (see: [[The Primacy]])

## First Three Signs
The First Three Signs of Affinity are all appearance-based. Only Cybertronians that display all of the First Three Signs are typically tested for later signs. Those who have the first three signs but none of the later ones are still sometimes considered “blessed by Primus.”
### 01 — Matrix-Blue Optics
The First Sign of Affinity is having a very specific optic color, which is a very saturated light blue. This is generally the most widely recognized and noticed Sign of Affinity, to the point that some people tend to forget the others even exist. 
### 02 — The Spark Sign
The Second Sign of Affinity is having a “pure” spark with a clockwise spin.
### 03 — The Color Sign
The Third Sign of Affinity is having abnormally bright and colorful innate paint scheme, or having an extremely unique paint scheme. 

## Later Signs
The last four Signs of Affinity are all significantly harder to test than the first three, either due to time commitment or requiring rare materials. Before the war, there were novelty clinics that would test all Seven Signs for a hefty price.
### 04 — The Sparklet Sign
The Fourth Sign of Affinity is that sparklets seem to grow faster in one’s presence. A very hard sign to test, and one that is typically ignored. Some argue that this sign should be the same sign as Hotspot Detection.
### 05 — The Sentio Metallico Sign
The Fifth Sign of Affinity is that one finds *sentio metallico* wet to the touch. 
### 06 — The Hotspot Sign
The Sixth Sign of Affinity is that one seems “giddy” when standing directly over a hotspot, whether they know the hotspot is there or not. Not able to be tested anymore come wartime, sometimes exchanged for the fourth sign in modern affinity tests.
### o7 — The Photonic Crystals Sign
The Seventh Sign of Affinity is that Photonic Crystals make a sudden “snap” or popping noise when placed directly over their spark.
