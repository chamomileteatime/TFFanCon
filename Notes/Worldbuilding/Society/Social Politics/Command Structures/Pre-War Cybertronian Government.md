## Political Parties
Political parties really only exist within the senate and in local authorities; ultimately they all submit to the authority of Primus, and are very keen to submit to the Prime and the Functionists. Political parties have no guaranteed representation in the Senate regardless of their population percentages; many politicians argue they're redundant.

#### ASCENTICONS
A radically progressive party; [[Shockwave]] was the most prominent member, although some less influential senators followed him ([[Senator Ratbat]]), and he had plenty of supporters between the Autobots and the Decepticons before the war broke out. [[Dominus Ambus]] was another prominent figure.

This party was not wholly anti-imperialist; it primairly campaigned for alternative energy and resource research, ambitious scientific endeavors, anti-functionism, colonization, Cybertronian expansion, economic and material diplomacy (at least, in theory), and more access to function exemptions and functionist accessibility reforms. It was also relatively atheistic, which ended up being one of the biggest sources of its controversy.

The Ascenticons had the largest pro-Decepticon population, and exhibited some beliefs that grew popular amongst Decepticons... namely, anti-functionism, an emphasis on research and development, opportunism, a weaker central government, significantly less bureaucracy, and more of a focus on physical authority as opposed to religious authority.

The name of the Decepticon movement is thought to have been taken from Decepticon ideology's associations with Ascenticon political philosophy, especially Dominus Ambus, [[The Ascetic Cybertronian]], and [[Towards Peace]]

#### REVERSIONISTS
An ascetic party associated with overt pacifism, conservative priorities, religious and social unity, and moderate economics. [[Dai Atlas]] and [[Star Saber]] were some prominent figures.

Reversionists were not necessarily pro- or anti-functionist initially; they viewed any radical change as a disturbance of the peace and believed that a neatly structured-yet-welcoming system of government that could slowly, incrementally change was what Primus had intended. Over time, they became more anti-functionist as party leader ideals aligned more with the idea of equal opportunity in spite of forged function.

Reversionists were more active in pushing legislation against ambitious research, overly-harsh policies, experimentation, execution and punishment methods, private ownership and the priority of the individual, and involvement with extraterrestrial bodies. Because Reversionists tended to dislike the idea of even reaching out to other species, they tended to be very protectivist and isolationist. 

Despite their conflicting beliefs regarding scientific research, colonization, and private research, Reversionists and Ascenticons often worked well together.

As war seemed inevitable, most Reversionists either opted to try to disappear from the war, or they assimilated into the Autobots.

#### EXALTATIONISTS
A socially conservative party; [[Senator Proteus]] was its most prominent member, and Shockwave's political rival. Exaltationists legislated against anti-functionism and encouraged liberal economics, a highly communitarian anti-individualist framework, an emphasis on adhering to one's role in the Cybertronian government, and restricting the social structure to a neat, efficient system in line with religious teachings re: Adaptus.

Not all Exaltationists were religious, but many of them had enough adherence to religion that their policies could be defended by looking to Primus's intentions with the functionist framework and the purpose of individual alt modes.

Exaltationists encouraged overwhelming positivity and satisfaction with one's own insignificance with regard to the functionist system. Before the war, most Cybertronians could either be considered moderate/unaligned or a member of this party; it had the most power in the Senate, just barely rivaled by Senator Shockwave, and tended to cooperate more with the reigning Prime (up until Optimus).
