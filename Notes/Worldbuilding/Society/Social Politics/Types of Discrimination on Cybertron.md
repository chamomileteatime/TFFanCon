## Size Discrimination
### Minicon & Minibot Discrimination
Discrimination against smaller-than-average size classes is rare on Cybertron, and defenses against this form of discrimination in particular are codified into law. Some amount of discrimination against Minicons and Minibots has however begun to appear within the Decepticon Army, often seeing these smaller-than-average Cybertronians as traitors to the Functionist norm, betraying their brethren to better fit in to Cybertronian society as an “acceptable” sort of abnormal development.
### Jet-Sized+ Discrimination
Discrimination against larger-than-average size classes is significantly more common than smaller size classes on Cybertron. It frequently takes the form of spaces not built for Cybertronians of larger-than-average size, making it hard for them to navigate as well as to find work. While the Decepticons have done much good in expanding accessibility to larger sizes, a lot of their improvement comes in the form of offering only standard-size and jet-size accommodations, rather than broad accommodations for all sizes. This is still an improvement over the Autobots, who still struggle to include more than standard-size accommodations and equipment anywhere. 
### Midweight Discrimination
The most common type of size discrimination on Cybertron, and in both armies. Midweights are seen as abnormal, and their unique anatomy and needs are often underdiscussed by the Cybertronian medical system. Midweights often are expected to pick on of the size classes they straddle, and to do their best to exist in that size class without making their differences too apparent.
