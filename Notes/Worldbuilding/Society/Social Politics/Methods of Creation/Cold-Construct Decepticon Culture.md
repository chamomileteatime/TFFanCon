# Cold-Constructed / Decepticon Culture
## Seeker-Unique Culture
*Note: Seeker-Unique Culture is an effort formed by Generation 2 Seekers following the start of the Cybertronian Civil War, seemingly as a propaganda or a moral effort.*
### Reasons
There are many reasons for Seekers specifically to have developed a unique culture within the Decepticon Army:
- A desire for their unique experiences to be recognized and valued by outsiders as well as the in-group.
- Romanticizing what makes them unique from other Cybertronians as a counter-movement to being perceived as “less than” other Cybertronians.
- A push for Trinebonds to be seen as equal to Amica and Conjunx bonds.
- A lack of privacy stemming from being mass-produced, leading to outsiders to see them as “promiscuous.”
- A Decepticon propaganda and/or moral effort. Seeker Nationalism.

### Naming Schemes
Generation Two Seekers were originally forged with very short, thematic names, such as “Sky” or “Thunder.” Modern Seeker names come in two halves: a “core” name, and a “descriptive” name. A core name is the name given at construction, and is never changed. A descriptive name, however, can be changed throughout a Seeker’s life. The core name can be either the first or second part, and can even switch locations during renaming.

Descriptive names, when changed later in life, are typically changed to:
- Describe an outlier ability (i.e., “Cracker,” or “Warp”)
- Are matched to their Trine (considered to be _particularly_ sappy)

### Sparkmerging
Seeker Trines are unique in that they are one of the few cases in which non-sexual sparkmerging is seen practiced culturally, the other being the process to bond Amica Endura. Unlike Amica, however, Trinebonds are forged stronger through repeated sparkmerging, regardless of sexual intent, making them as strong as Conjunx bonds. Technically Amica could regularly sparkmerge to strengthen their bond, but this is considered culturally abnormal.

Fostering a trinebond is typically done through both neural interfacing and sparkmerging. Non-sexual merging is common even amongst sexual Trines, as it is seen as a very relaxing process. It is not uncommon to see Trines spend their free time plugged into one another on a berth, chassis open; a fact which has greatly contributed to Seeker’s stereotyping as promiscuous. In reality, Seekers are even more private than other frametypes when it comes to sexual interfacing—they just don’t consider casual sparkmerging as inappropriate, although bearing a spark is still seen as a deeply private matter. The difference between walking in on a couple relaxing together in bed with little to no clothes and walking in on them during intercourse.
