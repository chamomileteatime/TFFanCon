# Cold-Construction
## The Spark
### Spark Splicing
The Spark of a Cold-Construct is obtained through a process known as “Spark Splicing,” in which the Spark of a living (typically forged) mech is agitated in a way that causes it to split into multiple sparks. This process is nearly always fatal. 

Affects of the Spark used for splicing will often translate into raised chances of said affects in its “offspring.” This is because the Spark is what contains CNA for Cybertronians, although random genetic mutations *can* happen. Below is a chart of what can and cannot be passed on through Spark Splicing:

| Trait              | Can Carry On | Random   | Overwritten |
| ------------------ | ------------ | -------- | ----------- |
| **Spark Type**     | **true**     | false    | false       |
| **Outlier Status** | **true**     | false    | false       |
| **0.1% Status**    | false        | **true** | false       |
| **Color**          | false        | **true** | false       |
| **Frametype**      | false        | false    | **true**    |

---

Contrary to popular belief, outlier abilities in reality can be more common in Cold-Constructs than in forged Cybertronians. This is a side-effect of the spark-splicing process in which abnormal abilities and mutations become more frequent the more often a spark-splice is reused to ignite new sparks. Eventually, the spark-splice will reach a point of overuse, where potential mutations will tip from outlier abilities and .1% to non-viable mutations, at which point the spark-splice will be retired from service. Many resources have been funneled into research on how to keep this golden 'mutation zone' on spliced sparks viable for as long as possible.

Another assumption that is prevalent is that the function of a progenitor-spark must match the function of any potential offspring, or run the risk of severe mental disturbances. Contrary to Functionist belief, there is no such thing as a “flier’s spark,” nor any spark variance between alt-modes at all. This superstition is still widely believed, and the art of selecting the “perfect” prog-spark is still widely practiced today.

	EUG: Eugenesis

	010: Relinquishment Clinic
	212: Hospital
	313: Private Collection
	414: Research Sample
	616: Criminal Punishment
Last Two Numbers: Current Phase of Spliced Spark 
	00 - No Known Mutations
	01 - Known Mutations, Pre-Golden Period
	02 - “Golden” Period
	03 - Retired
