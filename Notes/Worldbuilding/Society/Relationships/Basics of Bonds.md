# Basics of Bonds
## Forged Bonds
Forged Bonds are present from the second a cybernymph first extricates itself from a hotspot. Because of this, they are only present in forged Cybertronians, as the spark-splicing process does not allow for the creation of split-spark bonds.

#### Forced COmbiner-bonds
Unlike split-spark bonds, several attempts have been made to recreate the process through which combiner bonds are forged. While no one attempt has been able to fully recreate all aspects of a forged combiner bond, several attempts have resulted in somewhat functional combiner transformations, both from cold-construction as well as modification to non-combiner Cybertronians.
