# Thirteen Lineages
## Prima
Prima lineages are deeply concerned with their own appearance. They value themselves based on the opinions of others, hiding away the pieces of themselves they find unappealing. While both S-Pri and M-Pri are deeply concerned with outside opinions, S-Pri is prone to lying about the uglier sides of their identity, and acting in a way that they feel others will appreciate. M-Pri instead takes the angle of a creative, attempting to develop skills which others will appreciate them for, and discarding whatever does not gain them the praise they feel they are due. The counter-type, C-Pri, however, attempts to control this self-loathing by taking control of social-hierarchies, putting themselves in positions where they feel they cannot be judged.

### C-Pri — “The Commander”
#### Features — CT+ AT-
**Goal:** “My goal is to help these people.”
**Fault:** “I struggle in the face of negative attention.”

#### Description
C-Pri’s goal is to help people, which they achieve through control. C-Pri often offers their peers guidance, but will equally take over the situation if the peer does not follow it. C-Pri’s desire for control leads them to struggle with criticism, as it challenges not only their view of the world, but what they believe their value to others to be.

C-Pri prides itself on its hierarchical thinking. It can take many different forms, ranging from the more social approach ("I am more influential/less significant than you"), to the physical approach ("I am stronger/weaker than you"), to the personable approach ("I am more wanted/less wanted than you"), to the overarching "I have more value/less value than you." All of these end with the conclusion, "...and so you should trust me to guide you in the right direction."

This lineage is uncomfortable in positions of helplessness or in the absence of authority. Successful assertions of authority reinforces C-Pri's sense of security and provides a feeling of stability within their perceived hierarchy. C-Pri likewise has a tendency to come off as very paternalistic; in its effort to retain control without inviting negative attention, it takes a "I know what's good for you even if you don't" attitude, unlike the more authoritarian "my way or the highway" approach of signs like C-Lie.

#### Additional Information
*Enneagram correlations:* SO1, SX1, SO2, SO8
*Potential assignments:* **Prowl** (Core), Fortress Maximus, Optimus Prime

### S-Pri — “The Penitent”
#### Features — AT+ AP-
**Conviction:** “My value comes from other’s validation.”
**Fear:** “I fear losing the ability to gain validation from others.”

#### Description
S-Pri prefers to follow the words of others. They believe that love is the solution to all problems, particularly those of their own mind. S-Pri is a lineage that relies heavily on peers for their own self-actualization. Their actions often align themselves to those around them, acting on what they predict their peers to favor, rather than based on their own goals and convictions.

S-Pri is at its core an anxious lineage. Their fears often are the only part of themselves focused inwards, concerned about making themselves someone unworthy of external validation. This anxiety can be outwardly visible, or it can be a closely-guarded secret, if S-Pri feels that they would be judged for their troubles. S-Pri is a lineage adept at lying—often putting forward what others want to see and hiding everything else away.

S-Pri requires love and affection to function. They are easily slighted, and become dramatic when emotional. The peers of S-Pri can easily become a threat, should they stop providing the validation S-Pri needs. That being said, S-Pri is the least aggressive of the Prima lineages, instead coming across as fickle, abandoning those that fail to comprehend how deep their need for validation runs.

#### Additional Information
*Enneagram correlations:* SX3, SO4, SO6, SP6
*Potential assignments:* Hot Rod/Rodimus Prime

### M-Pri — “The Prisoner”
#### Features — PB+ Cm
**Presence:** “I need to present myself as someone to love.”
**Blind Spot:** “I fail to understand that people like me.”

#### Description
M-Pri believes in a secret standard which all people are held to. In this way, they are a prisoner to their own judgment, believing that their peers are constantly waiting for their next big mistake. They desperately want to be seen as someone others can love and appreciate, and are willing to cut away parts of their identity to better fit that mold.

M-Pri is a hardworking lineage. They find joy in pleasing others and building skills worthy of praise. However, this dedication relies on external validation, and collapses as soon as they feel their skills are not being properly enjoyed. M-Pri is constantly comparing themselves to their peers in all endeavors, and if they feel as if their own skills do not match the silently understood standard, they drop the niche entirely. Due to this, M-Pri can often be found picking up new skills frequently, but never quite mastering any of them. 

M-Pri does not comprehend that this “secret standard” is a creation of their own self-doubt. They can be just as aggressive as C-Pri in holding others to the standard that they believe is the norm. Being worse at M-Pri at their chosen skill can feel like a death sentence, as they are prone to passing their self-doubt onto judgment of their peers.

#### Additional Information
*Enneagram correlations:* SP1, SO3, SX3, SP3, SO6
*Potential assignments:* Red Alert

## Megatronus Prime
Megatronus’ lineages are all tied to a desire to see the world changed. They excel at seeing the big picture, and coming up with clever solutions to issues. Except for the countertype, M-Mga, Megatronus lineages thrive in groups, where they can spread their passions and ideals to those around them. 

### C-Mga — “The Insurgent”
#### Features — MI+ CT-
**Goal:** “My goal is to make you hurt like me.”
**Fault:** “I struggle to selfless help those around me.”

#### Description
C-Mga prides themselves on their unclouded and perceptive view of the world around them. This unclouded view of the world hurts them, and to “fix” it they will force others into seeing the world the way they do. If only everyone could see the hurt that they see, then people would stop hurting one another and start protecting each other instead.

C-Mga believes in a might-makes-right mentality. They will goad others into arguments and fights in an attempt to communicate with them. This is because they truly believe that they can beat down others into agreeing with their worldview, should they only try hard enough. Because of this, they often benefit from someone around them able to lead them away from bad decisions.

C-Mga's incredible confidence in its own perception of the world is reflected in its desire to see that perception realized by others. They are loud and passionate, striving to likewise beckon passionate reactions out of their peers. C-Mga seeks to draw out reactive emotions and loud, violent proof their worldview is indisputably true, making them both a dangerous enemy and a passionate ally.

#### Additional Information
*Enneagram correlations:*  SX1, SX2, SX6, SO8, SX8
*Potential assignments:* Overlord

### S-Mga — “The Warden”
#### Features — CN+ nr-
**Conviction:** “I believe goodness comes from helping others.”
**Fear:** “I fear being selfish.”

#### Description
S-Mga believes that goodness can be present in all people—but only if they are serving the needs of others. They are an emotional and passionate lineage, empathetic to the hurt of others in a way few can match. Like C-Mga, they want others to comprehend their worldview and agree with their perspectives, although their worldview focuses less on stopping pain and more on helping those who experience it.

S-Mga, like many other Spark Systems, wants to help those around them. The difference, however, is that S-Mga also understands the “big picture” of the world around them. Just being someone ready and willing to help is not enough for S-Mga, they need to push those around them to be willing to help as well. Only when everyone is willing to help each other does S-Mga feel that they have made any lasting positive impact on the world.

S-Mga struggles the most with the scope of their convictions. Aid only feels valuable if it’s offered by everyone, so their own assistance seems miniscule in comparison to the ideal they have created. Their greatest fear is that the help they work so hard to give—and convince others to give, comes from a selfish desire within them, rather than true altruism. The concept that they are not operating from true goodness and moral purity deeply concerns them, more so than any other lineage.

#### Additional Information
*Enneagram correlations:* SO7, SO8
*Potential assignments:*

### M-Mga — “The Architect”
#### Features — rf+ pr
**Presence:** “I need to present myself as someone striving for a better world.”
**Blind Spot:** “I fail to recognize and act on my own emotions in a vacuum.”

#### Description
What M-Mga wants more than anything is to be seen as the first person to go to on how to improve the lives of people everywhere. Like all Megatronus lineages they have an undistorted view of the world, and goals for how to fix it. This betterment can differ much more than other Megatronus systems, however. A M-Mga might sharpen their need for change into a passion for invention, or a love for entertainment. At its core, however, M-Mga still uses these passions as a vessel to communicate their worldview. 

M-Mga often tries to present themselves through good characteristics: kind, well-read, or highly intelligent. They may go so far as to lie about who they are or where they came from. M-Mga struggles with the idea that it is okay for them to openly be who they are in private, or even that they do not need to fix the world by themself. They put their ideals and convictions first, believing that who they are as an individual is not important outside of the goals they wish to achieve. 

There is no concept of a time for work and a time for play with M-Mga. Even at their most relaxed, their interactions with others is focused on betterment, not personal enjoyment. This can create an environment where M-Mga becomes internally isolated from its peers, while still engaging with them regularly.

#### Additional Information
*Enneagram correlations:*
*Potential assignments:* **Brainstorm** (Core), Getaway, Drift
