## Introduction
Drafted sometime just before the onset of the war, the Froidian Classification System was created to help Cybertronians make sense of themselves and their feelings/aspirations toward their functionist roles in society. 

Because many Cybertronians express discomfort toward the sort of loss of identity being reduced to their function can create, Classification Systems are a semi-socially acceptable way of exploring detailed individuality in ways that don't end up contradicting one's forged purpose.

Some people may be considered eccentric for labeling/typing others or making generalized statements about types, but it's not considered weird to "know" your base type. 

As the onset of the war began, and conflict spiraled into violence and uncertainty, Classification Systems gave some Cybertronians (especially pro-Decepticons) ways of understanding their behavior without fully shunning the Functionist system.

## The Basics
The Froidian Classification System (**FCS** for short) has a wide array of types. Each type contains three elements:
1. The **core** (A, B, C, or D)
2. The **auxiliary** (A number 1-7)
3. The **tertiary** (A number 1-7)

Each type then contains a set of "hidden functions"---because each aux/tert function has a complement, each type then has its own implied inferior functions.

Your core type can also contain a subtype defined by its adherence to Electromagnetic Spectrosophy;
1. **R** - Radioelectric
2. **I** - Infrared
3. **V** - Photoelectric
4. **U** - Ultraviolet
5. **X** - Variable
6. **G** - Gammaelectric

Therefore, while the most common, basic rendition of one's FCS type would be something like **B34**, typology enthusiasts can elaborate on their types to the extent of something like **Vx B(N-II) 3(2)4**. 

Astrology enthusiasts can take this a step further and include their astrological sign---so it wouldn't be complete gibberish to see someone with **Vx B3.2-4 nav** in their bio.

## Mainstream Core Functions
The core functions are relatively simple, and famously misunderstood. While the mainstream, workplace-typical theory has them set up as...

1. **A** - *The Delegate*.
A past-oriented decisionmaker who favors rigidity and dislikes chaos. Type As are loyal, hard workers, and they're especially good at drawing connections between past trends and present happenings. Type As thrive in structured workplaces or very technical professions, but struggle with future-planning, idea-making, creativity, and individuality. To a Type A, all people, all things, are factors---cogs in a system. They're good at detail-oriented analysis, not big picture analysis. They can deconstruct something and understand how it works, but they have a hard time building from the ground up.

2. **B** - *The Philosopher.*
A present-oriented thinker who is simultaneously interested in how things got to where they are, and what that means for future progress. A very big-picture thinker, an intellectual, and someone interested in creating new ways to elaborate on the present Cybertronian systems. Type Bs are very intuitive, can express emotions without compromising their function efficiency, and tend to even intuit useful functions before others can. Type Bs can be a little rebellions and individualistic, but ultimately they see the world in terms of the large-scale ideas and processes that make things the way they are, so of all people, they understand why things are the way they are, and how to improve them, not deconstruct them.

3. **C** - *The Harvester.*
A future-oriented visionary, stereotypically with close religious ties. Type Cs can appear as if they have their heads in the clouds, or even that they're not sure of what they're doing in the present moment, but this is primarily because they tend to be very steadfast, stubborn, patient individuals who work toward some ideal future they see ahead of them. To Type Cs, the past is not as important because the world is ruled by a few fundamental forces that ensure the future may be one way or the other. Type Cs are known to create longterm plans and seek to contribute to a world they may not be able to see one day. They tend to struggle with working with others, and they don't often understand certain directions, but put into the right position, Type Cs can be fantastic longterm workers dedicated to producing perfect results even if it means wading through delayed gratification.

4. **D** - *The Host*
An all-rounder, widely experienced, very synchronised with the people around them. If Type Cs struggle with people, Type Ds make up for it. Type Ds are not obnoxiously sentimental---they are not predisposed to more relationships. They are instead very good at reading and understanding why people are and how people are feeling. They may have good insight into navigating complex social situations and issues, and they may better serve their function by oscillating between a few similar jobs rather than honing their skills at one. Type Ds are the rarest types, and you seldom see them succeed, but if they do manage to prove themselves, they can be very well-known, very powerful people-people.

## Theoretical Core Functions
One important thing to note about the actual theory, is that each of the four types is comprised of a set of sub-traits:

### The Three Positions...

**First Position:** How do I assess my surroundings? What functions do I immediately employ to solve problems and understand the world I live in? These functions come in groups of opposites (Materials > Intuition, or Systems > Association)
- Extroverted: 
	- Intuition (big-picture analysis, creativity)
	- Materials (grasp of the physical world)
	- Systems (working with groups, synchronity)
	- Association (working with people, social manipulation)
- Introverted: 
	- Association (understanding the self and its beliefs)
	- Systems (a complex inner logical process)
	- Intuition (dreams, aspirations, etc)
	- Materials (memories, learned information)

**Second Position:** How do I relate my thoughts/feelings/findings to the way the world moves? How does my place in the world make sense?
- Extroverted:
	- **Past** ("Who am I?", casuality, destruction, tradition, sentimentality)
	- **Present** ("Where is my place?", involvement, activity, spontaneity)
- Introverted:
	- **Future** ("Where am I going?", planning, possibilities, goals)
	- **Eternity** ("Why am I?", meaning, significance, the view from a distance)

**Third Position:** What is my will? How do I put aspects of my mind into action?
- Monologue:
	- **Strong** ("I", self-assured, stubborn)
	- **Weak** (self-doubt, ambition, desperation)
- Dialogue:
	 **Strong** ("We", accommodating, patient)
	- **Weak** ("You", deferential, content)

### First Position
#### EXTROVERTED
Extroverted types rely on objective, provable information drawn from their surroundings. Types prioritizing extroversion do not have to be social; they just have to be focused outward.
Extroversion correlates with **(strong) dialogue**. Weak monologue type C is an exception.

**Intuition:** Extroverted intuition (a corkboard with pins and thread, abstract patterns in one's environment, hidden meanings, on-the-ground investigation and speculation, science, future-orientation)
*Main Types:* C(S), C(F), B(N), D(N)
*Subtypes:* C(S-II), C(F-II), B(N-II), D(N-II)

**Materials:** Extroverted material orientation (a bucket list of experiences and places to see, physical sensations, impulsivity, immediate surroundings, physical observations, past-orientation)
*Main Types:* A(S), A(F), B(M), D(M)
*Subtypes:* A(S-I), A(S-II), A(F-I), A(F-II), B(M-II), D(M-II)

**Systems:** Extroverted logical systems (teamwork, groupwork, leading the charge, explaining data, presentations, putting together everyone's ideas, application provides ideas, present-orientation)
*Main Types:* B(M), B(N), C(S), A(S)
*Subtypes:* A(S-I), A(S-II), B(M-II), C(S-II)

**Associations:** Extroverted social associations (social systems, morals, belief systems, compulsions, triggers, inhibitions, character flaws, urges, fears, eternity-orientation)
*Main Types:* D(M), D(N), C(F), A(F)
*Subtypes:* D(M-I), D(M-II), A(F-I), A(F-II), C(F-II)

#### INTROVERTED
Introverted types rely on subjective, speculative information deliberated on in their internal environments. Types prioritizing introversion do not have to be asocial or quiet; they just have to be more focused on how they process things, as opposed to what the outer world presents.
Introversion correlates with **(strong) monologue**. Weak dialogue Type A is an exception.

**Intuition:** Introverted intuition (gut feelings, abstract senses of self, internalized ideas, curiosity, internalized pattern-seeking, future-oriented)
*Main Types:* C(S), C(F), B(N), D(N)
*Subtypes:* C(S-I), C(S-II), C(F-I), C(F-II), B(N-I), D(N-I)

**Materials:** Introverted material orientation (looks to the past and what they've felt before, familiarity, principle of induction, grudges, precedent, past-oriented)
*Main Types:* A(S), A(F), B(M), D(M)
*Subtypes:* B(M-I), D(M-I), A(S-II), A(F-II)

**Systems:** Introverted logical systems (presupposed ideas and logical frameworks, self-assured knowledge and application, individualized internal train of thought, independent solution-building, idea first then application, present-orientation)
*Main Types:* B(M), B(N), C(S), A(S)
*Subtypes:* C(S-I), C(S-II), B(N-I), B(M-I), A(S-II)

**Associations:** Introverted social associations (personal beliefs, morals, fears, desires, attachments, inhibitions, compulsions, etc... especially one's place in society. eternity-orientation)
*Main Types:* D(M), D(N), C(F), A(F)
*Subtypes:* C(F-I), C(F-II), D(N-I), D(M-I), A(F-II)

### Second Position
#### EXTROVERTED
Types with first positions both associated with extroverted second positions are referred to as  **double extroverts**. For example...
- **A(S-I)** is (strong) extroverted materials and systems (present extroversion).
- **B(M-II)** is (strong) extroverted systems and materials (past extroversion).

**Past:** ("Who am I?", casuality, destruction, tradition, sentimentality, **materials**)

**Present:** ("Where is my place?", involvement, activity, spontaneity, **systems**)

#### INTROVERTED
Types with first positions both associated with introverted second positions are referred to as **double introverts**. For example...
- **C(F-I)** is (strong) introverted intuition and associations (eternal extroversion). 
- **D(N-I)** is (weak) introverted associations and intuition (future introversion).

**Future:** ("Where am I going?", planning, possibilities, goals, **intuition**)

**Eternity:** ("Why am I?", meaning, significance, the view from a distance, **associations**)

### Third Position
The third position monitors a Cybertronian's forward momentum, willpower, independence, action vs inaction, and deference to the world around them.
#### MONOLOGUE
Monologue refers to a volition that is self-centered or introverted. Monologue refers to the way one judges their own role/place in society, their ability to act, their actions, their identity, and their self-determination. It is egocentric and first and foremost worried about the self, but it is not necessarily going to come off as asocial or withdrawn.
Type C is all ruled by monologue.

**Strong:** Strong monologue is characterized by a great sense of stubbornness and self-assured comfort in one's identity/place/destiny/etc, usually whatever their second placement may be. Strong monologue in the future tends to present as ambition, strong monologue in the past presents as stubborn conservatism, et cetera. Strong monologue struggles with taking in new advice/ideas from other people and does not easily change its own identity.
**Weak:** Weak monologue is characterized by a fundamental doubt in one's identity/place/destiny/etc, usually whatever their second placement may be. It presents as pervasive insecurity, over/undercompensation for a perceived lack of volition, and a deep anxiety regarding their ability to act/function on their own.

#### DIALOGUE
Dialogue refers to volition that is group-minded or extroverted. Dialogue refers to the way one engages with hierarchies and the roles of others. It widens volition, action, role, hierarchy, and identity into a group discussion, readily taking insight and ideas from others and forming their own conclusions.

## Auxiliary and Tertiary Elements
The auxiliary elements are characterized by a type's greatest fixations, desires, future goals, and compulsions. 

Sometimes a type can get stuck in an **aux fixation** under stress, excitement, or sudden experience---aux fixations are not always bad, but can be indicative of bad mental health habits. **This is not a feature of the standard accepted FCS, as it encourages emotional fixation.**

The tertiary elements are characterized by a type's greatest vulnerabilities, weaknesses, and ultimate failures. 

Sometimes a type can get stuck in a **tert spiral** under stress, trigger, or sudden change---tert spirals are indicative of a person's vulnerabilities, as well as the sorts of things that benefit them the most in crisis. **This is not a feature of the standard accepted FCS, as it encourages emotional fixation.**

## Electromagnetic Spectrosophy
This is an additional set of typology used as an addendum; it consists of 6 types that define how one copes with the world. Some people may only have one primary type, others may have a primary and a secondary (i.e. Vx), others may have two competing primaries (i.e. VX). 

1. **R** - Radioelectric
2. **I** - Infrared
3. **V** - Photoelectric
4. **U** - Ultraviolet
5. **X** - Variable
6. **G** - Gammaelectric

They all exist on a spectrum, ranging from rigid-peaceful to vitalic-chaotic.

#### RADIOELECTRIC
Radioelectric energy is first and foremost worried about the preservation of the world around them. Beyond people, beyond groups---how do I keep my environment, my world, my home, stable and predictable? They tend to be withdrawn and sluggish, caring more for the sanctity of their surroundings than for themselves. They want to preserve, not change; they're reluctant to the idea of not knowing what their environment is doing or what'll happen next.

#### INFRARED
Infrared energy is first and foremost worried about proving its worthiness/proving it deserves to be incorporated into some final form of safety. Whether that's a safe job, a place in society that ensures safety, or getting the attention of someone---as long as it's something with the power to invite the infrared energy into a larger body, the infrared is drawn to it. It becomes rather conformist, almost appearing more rigid than radioelectric, in these efforts.

#### PHOTOELECTRIC
Photoelectric energy is first and foremost worried about avoiding missing out on experience, and on the joy/experiences/etc of the people immediately around them. Their focus is all on how things across time and space affect their immediate feelings/experiences... they're hedonistic, either sensorally or emotionally or both, and they tend to draw a lot of their energy from experiences and the people around them. They're certainly capable of being rule-followers, though, and their search for experience is not necessarily as unfettered as variable/gamma's.

#### ULTRAVIOLET
Ultraviolet energy is first and foremost worried about separating from a unity, opposite to infrared. Whereas infrared wants to be included, ultraviolet has always felt a deep separation from society/experience and wishes to find its true path beyond that. Ultraviolet energy is fixated on finding one's true purpose/meaning and believes that it cannot be found among regular society.

#### VARIABLE
Variable energy is first and foremost worried about preserving their own happiness and enjoyment of life as much as possible. This can present as a disregard for rules/stipulations, or a willingness to find loopholes/get around them if it means getting something they want out of a situation. Variable energy is chaotic and may often get mistaken for one of the other energy types, as it can latch itself onto anything so long as it believes it'll get something good out of it.

#### GAMMAELECTRIC
Gammaelectric energy is the least popular; it's first and foremost worried about preserving its own individuality and autonomy and experiencing the full forces of its own capabilities through sheer careless engagement in the world. It doesn't concern itself with social or one-on-one relationships nearly as much as it does with itself, though some gammaelectric energies (especially Gu/GU) may fixate on a specific group/person they obsess about getting power over. Gammaelectric energy can come off as overbearing, but it can also present as a deep urge to experience one's abilities for the sake of getting a grasp on them to use on/with others---it's just that the main motivation must be to preserve their own autonomy/individuality.
