# Nyon

### Basic Information
*Status:* Destroyed
*Controlling Faction:* N/A

*Notable Cybertronians:* Hot Rod

# Major Subdivisions
## Iridescent Heights
Central/Downtown Nyon. Densely packed.
### The Starway
The main street of Iridescent Heights. An environment not unlike the Strip of Las Vegas. A renowned vacation spot across all of Cybertron. Extremely popular amongst the nobility and Senate officials.

## The Rust Narrows
Slums on the southern edge of Iridescent Heights. Avoided by tourists smart enough to know better, a danger to those who don’t.

### The Acroplex
The Acroplex spans over the top of many skyscrapers in the city, and is therefore an eyesore across the entire city. Unknowingly a part of the lost titan Metroplex. A gathering place for many of the locals of Nyon. Home to one of the largest homeless populations on Cybertron, the well-to-do of Nyon have been lobbying for it’s destruction for millennia. Ironically the last structure standing in the ruins of what was once Nyon.

### The Descent
A smaller, rundown bar/casino on the border between Iridescent Heights and The Rust Narrows. 

## Hotspots
Nyon is home to one hotspot: Alyon. Due to it’s unique shape, the hotspot became a major tourist destination for particularly religious Cybertronians, eventually snowballing Nyon into the tourist destination it is today. Alyon is now completely closed to the public.

### Alyon
A hotspot in the shape of a giant hand. The center for research on spark formation and how different environmental factors effect the prevalence of abnormal sparks.
