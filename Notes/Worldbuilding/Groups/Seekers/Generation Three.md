# Background
The third generation of Seekers are a line of [[Cold Constructs]] made by the Decepticons during the Cybertronian Civil War. Unlike their predecessors, [[Generation Two]] Seekers, Gen 3 Seekers are made with significantly less raw material, and are generally preferred for stealth or non-combat roles. Their production was significantly rushed due to the war, leading to several cost and time-cutting measures in their design.

The Third Generation of Seekers was a project primarily run by [[Shockwave]], who was also the one to keep watch of the growing grublets.

## Spark Splicing
Unlike the previous generation of Seekers, as well as most other lines of Cold-Constructs, the third generation of Seekers are built from entirely from only one Spark Line. Thanks to their immortal spark, [[Starscream]] served as the perfect solution to the limited resources of the Decepticon Army, resulting in Generation Three Seekers having raised chances of Spark-based Outliers, as well as guaranteeing they would all be onlined with the Isomeric- sparktype, save one exception.

# Appearance
Color palette is generally mostly grey, with a faceplate color and a bright body color. Smaller than Gen 2 Seekers.
