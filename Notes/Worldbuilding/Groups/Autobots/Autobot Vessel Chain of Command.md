# Autobot Vessel Chain of Command
### Background
Unlike the Decepticons, most Autobots fulfill two purposes on a vessel—a standard day-to-day job to keep the station working, and a specific placement for battle. An Autobot can be a Fuel Requisitioner in their day-to-day life, and then serve as a sniper or demolitions expert on the battlefield. This leads to less on-ship infighting than their Decepticon counterparts, as there is not a tier of warriors sitting around waiting for their next battle to potentially cause issues. The downside of this approach is that it means that their warriors are generally less well-rested, and not as highly-trained as the Decepticon Army. A weakness that is made up for by having superior numbers.

## Captain
### Duties
- Maintaining the peace and crew moral.
- Being final say on issues regarding one or more departments under their command.
- Overseeing all departments, ensuring all departments are working towards a common goal.
- Communicating the state of the ship to superior officers or central command.

## Head of Personnel — Second-in-Command
### Duties
- Functioning as a Second-in-Command to the Captain.
- Filing and maintaining paperwork and paper trails for all other departments.
- Ensuring crew is fed.
- Putting together crew shifts.

### Underlings
#### Fuel Requisitioners
Duties: Ensuring the station has a proper amount of energon, ensuring the crew gets the right kinds of energon to each department.
#### Custodial Officers
Duties: Ensuring the upkeep and cleanliness of the station.

## Quartermaster — Third-in-Command
### Duties
- Keeping track of ship funds.
- Ensuring crew is paid for their time.
- Communicating budgeting constraints to other commanding officers as well as the Captain.
- Ensuring the crew is kept up-to-date on broader matters concerning the army as a whole.

### Underlings
#### Communications Officers
Duties: Hailing other ships, regularly checking a wide-range of frequencies for information.
#### Logistical Officers
Duties: Working with other ships as well as ports to ensure that necessary materials are acquired.
#### Cargo Technicians
Duties: Ensuring cargo is received and makes its way to proper departments.

## Head of Security
### Duties
- Ensuring crew is prepared for battle through assigning training exercises.
- Ensuring station safety, keeping a lookout for potential spies or saboteurs.

**Underlings**
##### Wardens
Duties: Maintaining the brig, ensuring prisoners are well kept.
##### Armorer
Duties: Maintaining the armory, ensuring arms are all accounted for before and after battles.
##### Security Officers
Duties: Ensuring the safety of the station.

## Chief Engineer
### Duties
- Having an understanding of all forms of engineering, being able to assist where needed.
- Having a complete understanding of the ship including past repairs and maintenance.
- Communicating maintenance needs to the Captain and Quartermaster.

### Underlings
#### Engineers
Duties: Depends on type of engineer, keeping the ship in working order.
##### Quantum Engineers
Duties: Maintaining quantum engines, addressing possible malfunctions.
##### Atmospheric Technicians
Duties: Ensuring that the ships maintains a liveable atmosphere.
##### Mechanics
Duties: Fixing parts of the ship as they break.

## Research Director (If Applicable)
### Duties
- Overseeing science-team research, ensuring it remains on-task.
- Communicating scientific-findings with Captain.
- Communicating Captain’s requests to the science team.

### Underlings
#### Scientists
Duties: Research and Development.
#### Roboticists
Duties: Drone upkeep & care.

## Chief Medical Officer
### Duties
- Ensuring the continued livelihood of the crew.
- Maintaining the Medbay.
- Leading search-and-rescue teams as-necessary.

### Underlings
#### Paramedics
Duties: Keeping track of crew vitals, transporting crew to the medbay in case of an emergency.

### Chemists (Sometimes split with RD, if applicable)
Duties: Manufacturing necessary drugs for the medbay, as well as potentially for the research team or other departments.

### Medics & Surgeons
Duties: Working shifts in the Medbay, ensuring crew makes it to regular check-ups.
