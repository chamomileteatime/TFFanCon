# Cybertron & Luna-01
---
## Decepticon Research Division Headquarters
**Location:** Outskirts of Vos
**Commanding Officer:** Shockwave

The Headquarters of the Decepticon Research Division. Supplying the front by way of spacebridge. Built under and attached to the old Cold-Construct factories of Vos, which were reactivated and altered to make the third generation of Seekers a reality. 

Shockwave operates the facility, as well as the labs underneath it, alongside a skeleton crew of Gen3 Seekers, primarily Sunstorm’s trine. The facilities and the attached Spacebridge were a major target for Autobot operatives on Cybertron, before the war. As it is now, Autobot diplomats have been pushing for access to the spacebridge for their own troops as well.

## Autobot Spec-ops Base
**Planet:** Cybertron, Under the ruins of Praxis
**Commanding Officer:** N/A

A base for the Autobot Special Operations Team, run by Prowl in secret. Mostly unmonitored. Prowl is technically the Commanding Officer of the base, except that he only shows up occasionally to see how things are progressing.

For all the fighting the Autobot operatives (primarily Elita-1) have done for the “final” functioning spacebridge on Cybertron, in turns out Prowl has been hiding one from them the entire time.

## Luna-01 Orbital Laser Superstructure
**Location:** Luna-01
**Commanding Officer:** Red Alert

An ancient base on the larger of Cybertron’s moon. Quite possibly the oldest sentient-built structure in the entire solar system. Currently occupied by Autobot forces.

Following the discovery several active and fully-functional hotspots on Cybertron’s largest moon, the Autobot Head of Security moved a dedicated team of personnel out to the main superstructure of the moon to secure it as an Autobot asset. The fact that Red Alert trusts no one with access to the moon other than themself and their two closest allies was a unforseen issue, but not surprising.
