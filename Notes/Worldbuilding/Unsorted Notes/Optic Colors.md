# Red Optics
Stereotypically a “Cold-Construct” optic color. Unpopular outside of Cold-Constructs. 
Red light can easily accidentally interfere with equipment due to raised amount of infrared light.
Easiest color to produce.
# Yellow Optics
The historic “default” optic color.
Opinions changed due to yellow being the color used for empurata optics, leading to the norm shifting to blue instead.
# Green Optics
Stereotyped as being the “Point One Percenter” optic color. Surprisingly rare.
# Blue Optics
The new norm. Exceedingly common due to the other two common colors (red and yellow) having strong stereotypes.
