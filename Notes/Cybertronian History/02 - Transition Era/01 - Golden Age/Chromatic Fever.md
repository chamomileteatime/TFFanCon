"**Chromatic Fever**" is the generic name for both a specific viral species (*Complecipleuravirus pandemicum*), and a specific pandemic spanning from the turn of the Golden Age to the Modern Era.

Chromatic Fever was responsible for wiping out the majority of life on Cybertron, including the Golden Age's long-reigning prime (see: [[Nova Prime]]), and is largely believed to be what devastated Cybertron's records of the Ancient Era.

While the first observations of Chromatic Fever's affects on the Cybertronian body appeared sometime around 7.0mil BCE, vaccine research did not officially begin for several hundred thousand years; a vaccine and treatment plan was formally introduced to Cybertron by [[Helminth]] sometime around 5.0mil BCE.

		- **GENUS:** *Compleuravirus* 
			- **SPECIES:** *Complecipleuravirus* *pandemicum* (classically known as [[Chromatic Fever]])

#### FURTHER CLASSIFICATION
*Complecipleuravirus pandemicum*'s specific species and presentation with regards to the pandemic is also occasionally written as ACST/ComPu/0531 (standardized to the modern system and calendar as "ACST-ComPu-0705-3BL") under the research classification taxonomy created by [[Pharma]] sometime after Helminth's passing.

Within this system, "ACST" refers to Chromatic Fever's "**A**cute **C**haotic **S**econdary (Anatomical) **T**erminary", the manifestation of transmorphological symptoms (Chromatic Fever's "pathological secondary anatomy" and namesake paint color changing) that will quickly and aggressively kill an untreated patient.

In this context, ACST will continue to refer to Chromatic Fever's physical presentations.

## Symptoms
#### ASYMPTOMATIC PRESENTATION


#### ONSET OF INFECTION
**~36-48 (40.00 +/- 7.569) joors after exposure**
One of Chromatic Fever's most infamous presentations---as well as its namesake---is the sudden change in a bot's exterior plating color. This is caused by the virus infiltrating the immune system's coding process and deregulating the programming and direction of the frame's epigidal nanites. This new color functions as a base color would, uncooperative with paint much like the frame's natural nanite coloration.

Chromatic Fever is also known to manifest a "shedding" of the epigidis. Cybertronians will observe this as a dusty, perhaps sandy, coating occasionally shedding or scratching from the exterior plating. As immune nanites fail to combat the misinformed, virulent epigidal nanites, they die off and leave the body. This falloff is liable to carry virulent nanites; there is a moderate chance that, when coming into contact with an infected Cybertronian's shedding, one would be infected as well. 

The Functionists have documented a sudden burst of confusion regarding one's alt mode or place in the taxonomy as a symptom of Chromatic Fever onset. This was debatedly Functionist propaganda meant to ward off political extremism in the wake of Nova Prime's passing.

#### EARLY STAGE
**~3-5 (4.00 +/- 0.236) orns after exposure**
Chromatic Fever carries with it most of the common viral symptoms: fatigue, a more sensitive EM field, and a need for more recharge and fuel consumption. The frame's internals rise in temperature as the body attempts to kill off the infected nanites. Very rarely patients experience nausea and fuel expulsion, insomnia/hypersomnia, clogged or inoperative vents, and more sensitive hearing.

Some Chromatic Fever patients, should their plating not immediately change color, may mistake their illness for a more common virus or rust fever.

If a patient has manifested the shedding symptom, they may also experience a profound itchyness or irritated sensation and exhibit some nonclinical self-harming behaviors. Excessive scratching, especially around the arms and legs, paired with other viral symptoms is one of the trademark signs of early-onset Chromatic Fever.

#### DEVELOPING STAGE
**About a quartex (1.953 +/- 0.08637) after exposure**
If the patient's joints have not begun to creak and prove difficult, they will at this stage. Once the virus's coding interruptions reach the transformation system, the frame experiences an acute, dramatic change in presentation.

The transformation system is naturally coded by the spark's CNA to allow for movement adjacent to whatever alt mode a Cybertronian is forged or constructed to have. Chromatic Fever prompts the frame not only to misunderstand the movements and armature necessary for its alt mode, but to reapply the frame's gestation protocols responsible for alt mode development in the sparklet growth stage.

As a result, the patient will begin to manifest odd, tumerous growths across their body, perhaps (but not always) vaguely resembling secondary anatomy recognized in other alt modes. The patient will find that its joints are weak and uncooperative and noisy even after being lubricated. The patient may find that some joints or parts may want to move in ways that would be inefficient for the frame at best, and harmful or fatal at worst. 

One of the most common fatalities at this stage is depending on whether the viral instructions affect any part of the frame's construction around the life cord---most commonly, the head, neck, and upper collar. It is vital to immediately address any issues with this area of the frame by way of surgery to protect the integrity of the brain module and spark.
