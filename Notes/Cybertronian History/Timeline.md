# Cybertronian Timeline
## Ancient Era
*Unknown—8mil BCE*
### Example Age
#### Notable Historical Events
1. Cybertronian Unification
#### Notable Physical Traits
More skeletal proto-forms alongside thicker armor plating and simplified designs.
#### Notable Cybertronians
Galvatron, Cyclonus, Scourge, Tailgate, Nova
## Transition Era
*8mil BCE—6.5 mil BCE*
### Golden Age
#### Notable Historical Events
1. Solidification of the Functionist Government
2. Original [[Ark Launch]]
3. Generation 2 Seekers are Made
4. Start of the [[Chromatic Fever]] Plague
#### Notable Physical Traits
Mixture of ancient and modern traits, not fully leaning to one appearance or the other.
#### Notable Cybertronians
Soundwave, Shockwave, Rewind, the Ambii, First-Wave Cold-Constructs
## Modern Era
*6.5mil BCE—2mil BCE*
### Example Age
#### Notable Historical Events
1. End of the [[Chromatic Fever]] Plague
#### Notable Physical Traits
Filled out proto-forms, more variance in plating thickness and more complex designs.
#### Notable Cybertronians
Pharma, Ratchet, Optimus Prime
## Warring Era
*2mil BCE—0 CE*
### Example Age
#### Notable Historical Events
1. [[02 - Cybertronian Civil War|Cybertronian Civil War]]
2. [[01 - Banishment of the Allspark|Banishment of the Allspark]]
#### Notable Physical Traits
Low resources for cold constructs, resulting in skeletal appearance and thin plating. Almost no forged Cybertronians. In the little forged being born there is a noticeable shift from pink to blue energon.

#### Notable Cybertronians
Fortress Maximus, Second-Wave Cold-Constructs
