## Serial Number Cheatsheet
### Alt-modes
001 — 200: “Archaic Alt-Modes,” Typically animals or other “simple” altmodes
201 — 399: Simple Ground Vehicles
401 — 499: Larger Ground Vehicles
501 — 799: Flying Alt-Modes
801 — 899: Scientific Alt-Modes
901 — 999: Military Alt-Modes
1000 — Beyond: Specialized Alt-Modes (Construction Vehicles, etc.)
### Castes
0X — Exotic Caste (Titan, Novelty, almost always unique)
04 — Erudite Caste (Scientific, Interstellar)
05 — Security Caste (Enforcer, Forensics)
06 — Civilian Caste (Standard, Medics, Rescue)
07 — Military Caste (Logistics, Warrior)
08 — Working Caste (Construction, Freight)
09 — Disposable Caste (Ornamental, Data)
00 — Undesirable Caste (Beast, Monoformer)
